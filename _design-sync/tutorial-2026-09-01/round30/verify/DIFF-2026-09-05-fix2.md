## 6 · Deviations, named
1. **The puck is lit by removing its own dim, not by a z-index.** `#guardPuck` is a `body` child at `z-index:92`,
   above the whole z90 cockpit, so no scrim inside `.tf-inner` can reach it. It wears a `.tour-pdim` of the same
   `rgba(32,10,36,.62)` on every beat and has it switched off on beat 6. Consequence: on beat 6 the whole home
   button reads lit, not just its pink disc — which is what the frame draws anyway (a round home button). The
   bubble and the ring still anchor to `.gpk-disc`, so every locked number is unchanged. The puck's 2px `#160510`
   border sits outside the `overflow:hidden` clip and stays undimmed; against a near-black border this is invisible.
2. **Beats 7 and 9 (1-based) anchor to FIXED chrome while their bubble travels.** The bubble and thumb ride the
   content on all three gesture beats, as instructed. On beat 5 (`godown`) the anchor `#tfToolsHint` is inside the
   scroller, so bubble, thumb, ring and target move as one — perfect. On beats 7 (`gohome`, anchor = the fixed
   guard puck) and 9 (`goup`, anchor = the fixed `#tfHudJourney` cue) the anchor cannot move with the content, so
   the tail drifts off its target while the finger is down. Beat 7 advances after only ~60px of travel, so the
   drift is small. Beat 9 advances only when the world reaches the journey (`st ≤ skyY + 60` ≈ 811px from home), so
   the bubble rides down and off the bottom before the beat hands over to the ending — the same handoff the engine's
   own fling produces. **This is the one place where "travels with the content" and "points at its target" cannot
   both hold, and it is David's call which he wants there.** The alternative is a one-line change: keep the fixed
   mount on the two beats whose anchor is fixed chrome, so the rule becomes "the bubble always travels with its
   anchor".
3. **No arrow on the settings beat, Skip reads "Skip", P1 carries the dim.** Unchanged from DIFF-2026-09-05 §E.
4. **Screenshots are not persisted as PNGs.** The Browser pane was HIDDEN for this whole session, so its screenshot
   tool times out and cannot write files. Everything above is therefore proven by DOM and computed-style assertion
   instead — including the paint-order probe, which is a stricter test of "does it read lit" than a screenshot would

## FIX-3 — "the bubble always travels with its ANCHOR" (2026-09-05, David's call on deviation 2)

Deviation 2 above put the call to David: on the two gesture beats whose anchor is FIXED chrome, does the bubble
travel with the CONTENT (and drift off its target) or stay with its TARGET? **His rule: the bubble always travels
with its anchor.** Built, and DERIVED per beat rather than authored — a beat is content-mounted when the thing it
lights lives inside `#tfWorld`, fixed-mounted otherwise, so a future gesture beat classifies itself the moment its
target is written.

| grep anchor | what it does |
|---|---|
| `function tourMount(B)` (`@SEC:TOUR`) | the rule itself: `B.gesture && tfWorld.contains(B.target())` → `"content"`, else `"fixed"`. No beat ids anywhere. |
| `function tourHost(B)` | keyed on `tourMount(B) !== "content"` (was `!B.gesture`): fixed-mount beats tear `#tourFlow` down and reset `TOUR.flowOff`, so bubble + tail + thumb + ring take the same fixed mount the reading beats use. |
| `DEV.tourAt()` | now reports `mountLaw` (what the rule says) beside `mount` (where the bubble actually is). |
| `// RAISE BEFORE MEASURE (FIX-3…)` in `tourPaint` | see §FIX-3.4 — a first-frame ordering bug the measurements caught. |

### 4 · One bug the measurement caught: raise before you measure

`.tour-litrel` gives a `position:static` target `position:relative` — which **activates any `top`/`left` the element
already carries.** `#tfHomeBars` measured 88.16 unraised and **102.06 raised**: the raise moves it 13.9px. Because
`tourPaint` placed the bubble *before* `tourPlace()` raised the target, beat 2 (strip) painted at **172** and was only
corrected to the frame's **186** by the rAF second pass a frame later — a 14px jump on David's phone, and invisible in
any preview where rAF runs. The order is now **`tourPlace()` (raises) → `tourBubblePos()` (measures the raised rect) →
`tourPlace()` (re-syncs the thumb, which reads the bubble's own top)**, with the rAF pass kept as belt-and-braces
instead of load-bearing. The table in §FIX-3.3 is the proof: every beat now lands on its locked number on frame 1,
with rAF frozen. Settled values are unchanged.
