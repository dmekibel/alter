Keep the visual language you established in Round 29. This round changes the FLOW, the PANEL FORMAT, and the COPY. Eight panels, in this order, ending inside the journey.

**The mechanic (unchanged from Round 29 unless noted):**
- The real screen stays rendered underneath. A dim covers it, and ONE region is cut out of the dim so it sits lit at its true position. Never a fake screenshot, never a zoom, never a card standing in for the real element.
- One panel per step. The panel is ONE flowing utterance in the guardian's first person. **No separate heading and subheading** — that split is dead.
- **Footer, new this round:** every panel carries `‹ Back`. The right-hand slot is either `Next ›` OR the gesture prompt (`Scroll down ↓`, `Scroll up ↑`), and where a gesture is shown, THE GESTURE IS THE BUTTON — performing it advances the tour, there is no Next to press.
- `I'll find my way` skip, quiet, on every panel.
- Each arrival earns a small mark.

**The eight panels.** For each: the screen it happens on, what is lit, the line, the footer.

1. HOME · nothing spotlit, whole screen lit
 "This is home. Your day, your tracker, and your practices, all on one screen."
 → `Next ›`

2. HOME · the day strip at the top (progress bars + activity icons)
 "This strip is your day as you planned it. It fills in as you live it."
 → `Next ›`

3. HOME · the big pink play button
 "This is the time tracker. Tap it whenever you start something, and I keep the time."
 → `Next ›`

4. HOME · the four shortcut tiles (Morning Stack, Breathe, Meditate, Night Stack)
 "These are your shortcuts. The practices and stacks you use most, pinned to home."
 → `Scroll down ↓`

5. TOOLS · the library grid
 "This is the tools library, where you can stack all the different practices. Breathing, stretching, meditation, and many others."
 → `Scroll up ↑ or tap home`

6. HOME · **three arrows at once**, one to each button: the planner, the garden, settings
 "The planner, the garden, and settings sit behind these three buttons."
 → `Next ›`

7. HOME · the JOURNEY bar at the top, lit
 "The journey sits above home. Scroll up."
 → `Scroll up ↑`

8. JOURNEY · the path · **this is the ending**
 "This is the journey. The manual for living well, taught one small step at a time."
 → TWO BUTTONS, not a Done: **`Start the first step`** and **`I will look around first`**
 The tour ends here, at the door of the thing that actually hands the user a first step. It does NOT end on home: nothing on home is glowing, and a closing panel pointing at an imaginary lit move was cut for exactly that reason (David 2026-09-04). "Start the first step" opens the journey's own doorstep pitch (self-chosen entry). "I will look around first" returns to home with nothing forced and nothing locked.

**Panel 6 is the one new drawing problem:** three arrows firing simultaneously to three buttons in different places, without the screen turning into a diagram. Show me how you'd solve it. If the arrows crowd, a single dim with three cut-outs and three short arrows is preferable to three long ones crossing the screen.

**Draw these states:** panel 1 (the resting format), panel 4 (a gesture footer), panel 5 (on tools, with the two-option footer), panel 6 (the three arrows), panel 8 (the two-button ending). Plus one frame showing `‹ Back` and the skip in place.

**What this tour deliberately does NOT do:** it does not explain WHY any room exists, does not pitch the journey, and does not teach how to use a tool. Those belong to the room's own doorstep and each tool's first run. The tour teaches WHERE. Keep every panel to the one utterance given.
