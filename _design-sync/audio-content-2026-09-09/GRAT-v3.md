## The shape (one draft; every line is its own clip; the composer picks by the CONTEXT LEDGER, see graph/engine-context-ledger.md)

**OPENER, exactly one or none**
- solo (nothing before it): `Close your eyes, and let your whole body relax.`
- in a stack, eyes still open (after stretch or breath only): `Let your eyes close.`
- in a stack, eyes already closed and body relaxed: nothing. Straight to the first ask.

**FIRST-TIME ONLY** (S.tools.use.gratitude == 0): `You'll think of a few things you're grateful for, one at a time, slowly. Then you'll stop thinking, and just feel it.`
**OCCASIONAL** (fewer than 5 runs, or more than 14 days since the last): `A few things, slowly. Then just the feeling.`
**REGULAR:** no explainer.

**THE ASKS, in order; the slot decides how many** (1 at 30s · 2 at 45s · 3 at 60s · 5 at 90s and up; a stack never goes past 5)
1. `Think of one thing you're grateful for. It could be something small, like a friend's smile, or the sun on your face. Or something big, like someone in your family.`
2. `Now another one. Something you'd normally take for granted. Like hot water this morning, or your eyes opening. Find your own, and feel it for a second.`
3. `Now something you're glad to be free of. A problem that passed you by. That counts too.`
4. `Now one from your past. A person who was good to you, or a place you got to be.`
5. `One more, and make it new. Something you've never said thanks for. It takes a moment to find.`
Screen line under each ask, rotating: `Silently, in your own words.` · `Slowly. Feel each one before the next.` · `Feel it in your body.`

**THE FEELING** (always): `Now stop looking for things. Feel the gratitude itself. Let it sit in your chest, and let it grow on its own.` (longest hold)

**WITHOUT A REASON** (always, David's last step): `Now try something. Feel grateful with nothing to point it at. Just the feeling.` then, first-time and occasional only: `Those were practice. This is the skill.`

**FIRST-TIME ONLY, after the feeling:** `If you didn't feel much, that's normal. It wakes up with practice.`

**SOLO, LONG (custom dose past 5 min) — the progression, inserted between ask 5 and THE FEELING:**
- `Keep going. Fresh ones, each time you do this. Use your head to find them.` (then the asks continue from a deeper pool: more taken-for-granted examples, the body, the past)
- `As you name each one, feel it rise in your chest.` (Stutz rung 2: feel while naming)
- after WITHOUT A REASON: `Whenever a dark thought starts today, do this again. Two things, then the feeling.` (Stutz's cue rule, the daily-life bridge)

**CLOSE**
- in a stack: `Stay with that a moment longer.` (the next act's opener does the bridging)
- solo: `Open your eyes when you're ready.`

**SOLO CARD, before Begin (screen, replaces the paragraph David killed):** title `Grateful Flow`, one line `Five things, slowly. Then just the feeling.`, a small credit line `a tool from Phil Stutz`.
