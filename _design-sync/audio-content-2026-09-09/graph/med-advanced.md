## THE DE-DUP MAP — what each block owns
Three of these blocks could all become "rest as awareness" mush. They are split by MECHANISM, and each move lives in exactly one:

| `being` | The DOER and effort. Adyashanti: relinquish, let be, the bait, the non-object return. | field mechanics, noting, any object |

The bait line (the mind offering one more thing to work out first) appears in `being` as a pull out of rest and in `look` as a trade for an easier question. Different jobs, deliberately different wording. The two never share a lane.
