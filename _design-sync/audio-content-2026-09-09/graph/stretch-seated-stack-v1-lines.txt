S| First, we'll wake up your body with a few easy stretches. A couple of simple movements is enough to change how the whole day feels.
B| Sit tall, near the edge of your seat, feet flat on the floor. Do everything very slowly, and avoid any movement if it causes pain.
S| Reach both arms up toward the ceiling. Hold them there, breathe out, then let your breathing return to normal.
S| Bring your arms down, then roll your shoulders up, back and down. Go big and slow, a few times.
S| Tilt your right ear toward your right shoulder, face forward. Keep both shoulders down and let your head's weight do the work.
S| Now tilt your left ear toward your left shoulder. Let the same weight do the work.
S| Turn your head to look over your right shoulder, body facing forward. Keep your chin level, only as far as feels easy.
S| Now look over your left shoulder the same way. Keep your chin level.
S| Reach both arms out in front at chest height, palms facing each other. Push them forward and round your upper back.
S| Lower your arms, then clasp your hands behind your back, arms straight. Lift your chest and open your shoulders.
S| Let go, and reach both arms up. Hold your right wrist with your left hand, lean to your left, and feel your right side stretch.
S| Now hold your left wrist with your right hand. Lean to your right, and feel your left side stretch.
S| Bring your arms down and put your hands on your lower back, fingers pointing down. Arch back gently and look slightly up, only as far as feels easy.
S| Come back upright. Put your left hand on the outside of your right knee, and use it to turn your upper body to the right, hips facing forward.
S| Now put your right hand on the outside of your left knee, and turn to the left. Hips stay facing forward.
S| Cross your right ankle over your left knee. Sit tall and lean forward a little, until you feel the stretch on the outside of your right hip.
S| Now cross your left ankle over your right knee. Lean forward a little, until you feel the stretch on the outside of your left hip.
S| Uncross your legs, feet flat on the floor. Keep your toes down and raise both heels, so you're up on your toes, then lower them slowly, a few times.
