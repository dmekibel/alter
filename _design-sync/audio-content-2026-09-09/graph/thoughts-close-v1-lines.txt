Now let go of the breath. Let your mind rest as it is.
Thoughts will come. You don't need to stop them.
When you notice you're caught in a thought, quietly say to yourself, thinking. Then let it pass.
Your mind is like the sky. Thoughts are clouds passing through it. The sky doesn't have to do anything about them.
Feel the space inside your body. Now feel the space around you, in the room.
Feel that the space inside and the space outside are one continuous space.
Rest there for a while.
Now come back to your body. Feel its weight again. Feel your feet against the floor.
Notice the sounds around you.
When you're ready, slowly open your eyes.
