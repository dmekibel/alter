Now scan down through your body, from the top of your head to your toes.
Notice where it feels comfortable and where it feels tight. Just notice.
Take your time. Move down through the face, the neck, the shoulders, the chest, the belly, the legs, the feet.
Now bring your attention to your breath. Breathe normally.
Feel where the breath is most obvious. The nose, the chest, or the belly.
Follow one breath in. And one breath out.
When your mind wanders, and it will, gently come back to the breath.
