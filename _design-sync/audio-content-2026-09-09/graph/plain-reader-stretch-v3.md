## CHECK 3 — THE SAFETY ROWS

**No. They set a standard and hand back a volume knob. Neither gives an exit.**
Row 1 offers one action, "ease off," which means turn it down, not come out. Row 33 offers "back off," the same verb class. Nothing in either row tells a person what to do when something actually hurts: no come out of it, no skip this one, no stop the session, no the next move arrives anyway so let this one go. For a 66 year old that gap sits directly on top of the three rows with real physical exposure, none of which offers a support or a skip: row 26 (single leg balance in a lunge), row 32 (both arms overhead on the toes), row 34 (kneeling on a hard bedroom floor). Row 33 additionally misplaces the listener before it advises them.
