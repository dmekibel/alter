## 2. THE ABSENCES

**1. The set never tells the listener to open their eyes, except as an emergency.**
The only unconditional eyes-open instruction in 110 lines is **close 3**, one of three pool lines. If the engine plays close 1 or close 2, the sit ends with the listener sitting in the dark with no instruction to come out. Everywhere else "open your eyes" appears — feel 1/2/3/4/5, look 3/4/5, open 5, bliss 7 — it is the distress exit. **Consequence:** in this set, opening your eyes means something went wrong. That inverts the source rule (`KB-SWEEP` E2, HS:L5: eyes-open is a first-class starting option, not a fallback), and there is no eyes-open on-ramp anywhere — settle's entry mandates closure ("let your eyes close on the next out-breath"). Two fixes needed: close 3 becomes mandatory-last rather than a pool member, and settle needs an eyes-open option.

**2. The second arrow is absent from the entire set.**
`SHIN:§3`, cited in the sweep for `note` (move 15), `feel` (move 7), `listen` (correction) and re-anchor cue 15. No line anywhere teaches noticing the reactive layer stacked on the first — irritation at the irritation, frustration at having wandered. It is one of Shinzen's three named core drills and the most transferable move in the library. Not taught once.

**3. Flow is absent.** `SHIN` Focus on Flow / Way of Flow — riding pulsation, expansion, movement, so a stuck or painful sensation dissolves into energy. The set has "notice whether it holds steady or changes" four times (breath 7, note 3, feel 4, bliss 3) but never change ITSELF as the object. One of the Five Ways, no rung.

**4. `embody` has no capstone.** Blackstone's own final move (`BL:L47–49`, sweep moves 16–18) is the recognition that the space outside the body is continuous with the inside, then repeating it eyes open. embody 9 gathers the whole body and stops. The block's entire effortless tier is missing, and it is the richest single source in the sweep.

**5. `count` teaches the mechanic and never the reward.** `HS:L17–19` — the felt "give" or softening that follows each exhale, the sense of space the count exists to reveal — is the actual target; the number is scaffolding. count 2 is about the number's length, count 3 about the ribs. Four lines, and the payoff isn't one of them.

**6. `scan` and `embody` carry no exit, at all.** Both are named trauma-sensitive in `KB-SWEEP` E2. Between them, 15 lines of sustained body inhabiting — scan 4 tells you to stay inside a gripped place — with no off-ramp in any line or either entry. `feel` and `look` are over-supplied with exits; these two have zero. And note that cutting bliss 7 removes bliss's only exit, which is why its last sentence stays: TMI Stage 8–9 names destabilizing joy as a real risk.

**7. Pain has one line in the whole set, in an advanced block.** being 8's "If something hurts, that's a different thing, so move and settle again." The sweep's `scan` correction (don't ask the user to sit inside pain in a basic scan) is unrepresented where it belongs.

**8. Turn Towards / Turn Away is never named as a choice.** Turn Away appears four times as a rescue (feel 3, open 5, note 4, bliss 4); turn-towards appears as feel 2/4. The source's load-bearing framing — *both build the identical skill, neither is more advanced* — appears nowhere, so every rescue line reads as a consolation.

**9. Re-noting when flooded** (`SHIN:§4` — re-note the same object slowly to slow the pace down) has no rung. Zooming is half-covered (out: open 6, breath 11; in: breath 9, scan 6).

**10. Four stacked entries claim a state the stack never produced.**
- **feel-STACKED:** "You just let the whole body go soft" — no block in the set softens the body. `scan` explicitly refuses to ("you are not here to loosen it").
- **free-STACKED** and **bliss-STACKED:** "your breathing is already paced" / "has already settled into its own pace" — both breath blocks forbid pacing it (breath 1: don't steer; count entry: never stretch a breath to fit its number).
- **embody-STACKED:** "So far you have been attending to the body from the outside" — false if `scan` played, since scan 3 teaches inhabiting by name.
A stacked entry is a claim about what just happened. These four are guesses.

**11. `free`'s cold entry doesn't work cold.** "Let go of whatever you've been holding on to" has no antecedent as a first block. Every other block's cold entry stands alone; this one should be marked stack-only.

## 3. THE CONVERGENCE FAILURES

Read as one body of work, applying law 5 strictly — *which teacher's strength does this line carry?* Traditional-sounding is the target (GUIDED-VOICE REGISTER LAW); ownerless is the failure. Five lines name no move:

1. **settle 5** — "The more often you sit down like this, the sooner your body starts doing this part on its own." A fact about future sits. No act available now, no teacher's strength; any app's onboarding could say it. (Cut above.)
2. **bliss 7** — "Stop the work, and go on feeling it without managing it." Abstraction in plainer clothes; nothing an observer would see change. (Cut above.)
3. **free 4** — "There's no result at the end of this, and nobody will ask you what happened in it." Reassurance with no owner, and its block already said it twice. (Cut above.)
4. **scan 4** — "A tight place that gets noticed will often let go later, on its own." A promise about the future dressed as permission. The line's owned move is "read it like every other place"; this sentence is the generic wellness beat attached to it.
5. **watch 1** — "You do not have to describe it or explain it to yourself." Permission with no act, in a line whose real move (words or a picture, then watch it to its end) is already complete without it.

**The set-level convergence problem is not a line, it is a move.** *Inhabiting* — get inside it, be in there and not next to it, a different act from observing — is taught in **settle 3, breath 10, count 3, note 3, scan 3, listen 4, watch 3, feel 2, being 5, heart 2, bliss 2, look 3, and the whole of embody.** Applying the move to a new object is legitimate variation. **Re-defining the distinction each time is not**, and four lines re-define it in near-identical words (scan 3, listen 4, being 5, embody entry). Blackstone's distinction was made canon so the app would teach it *once, explicitly*. Taught twelve times, it stops being a distinction and becomes the house tic — the one thing a listener will notice is ours, for the wrong reason. Three of the cuts above (settle 3, being 5, and the scan 4 subtraction) are the start; the stack warden needs a standing rule: **one inhabiting definition per sit, applications thereafter.**

## 4. THE THREE LINES TO SHOW THE FOUNDER FIRST

**1. open 8** — *"If you had to go looking for an answer, the sharpness has drained out and only the comfort stayed. That is the most comfortable way there is to lose a sit."*
This is TMI's subtle-dullness correction, which the sweep names as the single highest-value item in the whole ladder, and it arrives with a self-test a person can actually run with their eyes closed (ask what you can hear, and answer it properly). No meditation app teaches the failure mode that feels like success.

**2. open 3** — *"Both happen in about half a second, which is why they usually go by unseen."*
Harris's precision and Headspace's permission fused into one action, which is law 1's whole prize: it names the exact latency, gives the two things their names (leaning in, tightening against), and turns an invisible reflex into something catchable. One action, two teachers, no hedge.

**3. bliss 6** — *"It shows up in the breath first, as a small catch on the way in."*
This is the answer to the round that killed all four Spero cues. The abstraction was "don't grasp at the good feeling"; this line found the ACT underneath the model and gave it an observable a half-awake person can check. It is the clearest evidence in the set that the method corrected a real, dated failure rather than restating it in plainer words.
