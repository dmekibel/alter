Name a few things, slowly. Then stop the words and stay in your chest.
Think of one thing you are grateful for. It can be small, like a smile of a friend, or the sun hitting your face.
Or big, like someone who waited up for you.
Now one you would only notice if it stopped. Hot water this morning, or your key turning in your own door.
Now something bad that missed you. A pain that stopped by lunch, or a message you dreaded that never came.
Now go back years. Someone who stayed with you on a bad night and never mentioned it again.
Last one, and it is the biggest. You woke up this morning. You are alive.
Those were the logical reasons. They remind your body what gratitude feels like.
Now feel that same gratitude with no reason under it.
Put your hands on your heart if you like.
If you felt almost nothing, that is normal the first time. It builds every time you come back.
Keep going, and hunt for ones you have never named before. It is supposed to take a little effort.
As you name each one, wait until you feel it in your chest before you go to the next.
Later today, the first time you catch yourself replaying an argument, name two things and go to the feeling.
Stay with that, and keep breathing the way you are.
Open your eyes when you are ready. You made that feeling yourself, out of a few plain facts.
Silently, in your own words.
Slow enough to feel it before the next one.
