# ROBBINS' ACTUAL VOICE CUES — the gratitude segment (extracted from meditation-scripts/style5-robbins-priming.txt, 03:34 to 07:12)

David: "Tony Robbins said it differently. He said something like, imagine like you're actually there or something. Try to find all of his voice cues, because that could help."
These are his MOVES, listed so a writer can amalgamate them in our own words. Technique is free, expression is owned: none of his sentences ship.

## The posture move (03:34)
Both hands physically on the heart. Feel the heart. Breathe into it. The hands go back to the lap when the segment ends (07:04), which is what makes the block feel closed.

## The gift frame (04:08)
The heart was given to you, you never worked for it, as long as it beats you live. Gratitude arrives BEFORE any personal memory is requested. (Our ASK-5, you woke up and you are alive, is this move moved to the climax.)

## THE CENTRAL MOVE — step into the moment (04:43, repeated 05:45, 06:19)
Remember a moment you could feel deeply grateful for. **Step into that moment right now like you were there, and feel grateful for it now.** This is the one David remembered. It is the whole mechanism for turning a named item into a felt one.

## The sensory re-entry (05:13)
**See what you saw, hear what you heard, feel what you felt, now, as if you are there.** Three channels. We use ONE channel per cue, never his triad (the rule-of-three conflict in KB-SWEEP-robbins-priming §F).

## Size freedom (04:37, repeated 06:21)
A little moment or a big one, it does not matter. Any age, any stage of your life. (This is where David's "small like a friend's smile, or big" comes from.)

## The widening on the third repeat (06:30)
The moment, the person, or the situation. Even a coincidence that led to something important. Then the named domains: career, relationship, life.

## Compression on repeat
Moment one gets the full instruction, about 67 seconds. Moment two gets the same move in half the words. Moment three adds only the widening. He never announces a segment and never summarises one.

## What we do NOT take
His exhortation energy, "I want you to", his abstractions (beautiful, energy, blessed), his triads, and the healing and white-light material. Our register is hushed and plain per the GUIDED-VOICE REGISTER LAW.
