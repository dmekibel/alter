**Split, wording preserved on both halves (2 shipped clips retired, 4 new clips):**
- `Soften your forehead, and unclench your jaw` → `Soften your forehead` + `Unclench your jaw, and let your teeth come apart`.
- `Soften your chest, and your belly` → `Soften your chest` + `Let your belly go soft, and let the next breath fill it`.
- **Why:** two body parts in one 2 second beat means the listener acts on the second and drops the first. Both halves keep their shipped wording, so this is a re-cut rather than a rewrite; `gen-voice.py` banks these as the joined string only (`add(un(a)+", "+un(b))`), so it is four new clips either way.
- **The cheap fallback:** at short doses the two shipped PAIRED clips are exactly the compressed form of the four split ones, so a short chain can play the originals and a long chain the splits. See the drop order in §4.

**Replaced:**
- `One mindful moment, just be here, now` → retired. Reason in §2.

**Not written, deliberately (empty slots):**
- No mechanism or teaching line anywhere in the chain. Yapko's own PMR script contains none, `PMR_BEATS` puts its explanation in the intro card, and this tool is physical instruction only.
- No eyes cue inside the chain; the opener owns the eyes, and in a stack where they are already closed nobody needs to be told again.

## 4. WHAT THE BUILDER NEEDS (three notes, all real)

**a) THE RELEASE NEVER ACTUALLY PLAYS IN THE STACK.** `composeStackSegs` computes `cSpare = t.secs - cN*(PK.somatic + PK.speechEst)` and splits the leftover between the group rests and the final release. With the shipped 8 cues:

| relax slot | cSpare | group rest | final release |
|---|---|---|---|
| 45s (5 min band) | 0s | none | **2.0s** |
| 60s (10 min band) | 10.4s | 3.5s | **3.5s** |
| 75s (15 min band) | 25.4s | 8.5s | **8.5s** |

`PK.somaticRelease` is 45s and is never reached at any band, which matches `reality-morning-stack.md` exactly. The technique's defining beat is missing wherever the tool actually runs. Copy cannot fix this: either the release is reserved off the top before the leftover is distributed, or the relax slot goes to 90s. Flagging, not solving.

**b) THE REST LINES NEED A DOSE GATE.** R1 to R3 are spoken lines, so each costs its own ~4.2s of speech. At 45s and 60s they do not fit. Drop order as the dose shrinks, from the bottom up: optional rungs (neck, hips) → rest lines (boundary goes silent, which is today's behaviour) → re-merge the split cues back into the two shipped paired clips. The release line is never dropped.

**c) MARK THE RELEASE EXPLICITLY.** Today `_pk:"release"` lands on whichever cue is LAST. The close must be spoken AFTER the long silence, so the release has to be tagged on cue ★ rather than inferred from position. Variant B additionally needs a squeeze hold of about 5 seconds, which the current `PK` grammar has no kind for (`somatic` is a hard 2.0s floor and ceiling); Yapko specifies 10 to 15 seconds, `PMR_BEATS` uses 5.

## 5. THE COLLISION CHECK (the exact check run)

Script: `graph/relax-v1-collision-check.py` (re-runnable). For every relax line against every meditation line it computes (i) exact match on `re.sub(r'[^a-z0-9]','',s.lower())` — the same normalisation `composeStackSegs`' own `usedTxt` dedup uses — and (ii) content-word overlap `len(A&B)/min(len(A),len(B))` on stop-word-stripped tokens, which is the identical metric `copy-density.py` uses for its ECHO check, flagged at the same 0.60 threshold. Four corpora compared: the two new meditation drafts and the two live app.js stores.
