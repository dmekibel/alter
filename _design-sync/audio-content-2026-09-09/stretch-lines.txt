Fold forward. Hang heavy and let the neck and arms go.
Roll up slowly. Stack the spine, head comes up last.
Roll your shoulders back. Big and slow, a few times around.
Drop one ear toward your shoulder. Let the weight of your head do the work.
Now the other side. Just as slow.
Look over one shoulder. Chin stays level, only as far as feels easy.
Turn the other way. Same speed, and let the shoulders stay put.
Tuck your chin to your chest. Hold it there and lengthen the back of your neck.
Pull one arm across your chest. Hold it, and feel the shoulder open.
Switch arms. Same easy hold on the other side.
Bend one arm behind your head. Guide the elbow gently with your other hand.
Bend the other arm. Keep your ribs down as you reach.
Pull your fingers back. Arm straight, palm facing away from you.
Now turn that hand down. Same arm, and pull it gently toward you.
Now the other wrist. Fingers back, arm straight as before.
Turn that hand down too. Same wrist, and ease it toward you.
Spread your fingers wide. Then make a soft fist, twice more.
Press your palms forward and round your back. Open the space between the shoulder blades.
Clasp your hands behind you and lift your chest. Open the front of the shoulders.
Reach up and lean to one side. A long stretch down your ribs.
And lean the other way. Keep breathing into it.
Twist slowly to look behind you. Let your eyes lead the turn.
And twist the other way. Easy, all the way around.
Step one foot back into a lunge. Back heel up, press your hips forward.
Step the other foot back. Sink the same way, chest stays tall.
Set one heel out in front. Hips back, feel the back of the leg.
Change legs. Other heel out, hips back again.
Step back and press your heel down. Back leg straight, feel the calf.
Swap feet. Same heel press, same slow breath.
Rise onto your toes and lower. Reach both arms up as you rise.
Sit down on the floor. A chair is fine if that is easier.
Bring the soles of your feet together. Let your knees fall out to the sides.
Lean forward over your feet. Go slow, and fold from the hips.
Cross one ankle over the opposite knee. Sit tall until you feel it in the outer hip.
Cross the other ankle over. Sit up tall again and breathe.
Stretch both legs out in front. Pull your toes back toward your knees.
Fold forward over your legs. Knees can bend, let your head hang.
Hug one knee and pull it across your body. Sit tall and turn toward that knee.
Switch to the other knee. Keep the spine long, turn the other way.
Lie down on your back. Let the floor take your weight.
Pull one knee to your chest. The other leg stays long on the floor.
Change knees. Same easy pull, shoulders stay down.
Now both knees in. Wrap your arms around your shins.
Open your arms out wide. Palms up, resting on the floor.
Let both knees fall to one side. Turn your head the other way.
Roll the knees the other way. Keep both shoulders on the floor.
Lift one leg toward the ceiling. Hold behind the thigh, keep the knee soft.
Now the other leg up. Same hold, and let your head stay down.
Let your legs go long. Arms by your sides. Stay here and breathe.
