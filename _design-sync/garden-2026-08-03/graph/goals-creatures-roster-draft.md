# GOALS = CREATURES — roster + flow draft (2026-08-10, pre-skeptic)

Direction locked by David: sub-goals build the HABITAT, completion brings the RESIDENT. Trinity: practices grow plants, virtues keep flames, goals bring animals. Tiers already locked: quest / venture / saga.

## THE TIER MAP
- QUEST (small, days-to-weeks): one KOI joins the pond at completion. No habitat build (too small); the pond IS the quest ledger, it just fills. Koi palette: pearl, cream-gold, lilac, blue-black ink-spot. NEVER red-orange (world law; golden-apples precedent). The pond itself is either pre-existing or the first venture you build.
- VENTURE (medium, weeks-to-months): a common creature moves in. Habitat = 3 build stages (boat grammar s1-s3).
- SAGA (rare, months+): a one-of-a-kind resident. Habitat = 4-5 stages, grander.

## RESERVED SPECIES (the virtue emblems, unearnable via goals)
lion, bee, swans, owl, dove, cat, hummingbird, hare. If one of these ever appears alive, it came from the virtues system, never from a task. (Open conflict: rabbit vs reserved hare; duck vs reserved swans; songbird vs reserved dove/hummingbird. Skeptic adjudicates whether "close cousin" species read as violations.)

## VENTURE ROSTER (creature: habitat build stages: motion once resident)
1. Songbird: birdhouse (post → box → painted roof): perches, occasional flit between two trees, dawn song.
2. Squirrel: canopy drey + rope run (rope → drey → feeder): runs a fixed branch loop in the grove crowns.
3. Frog: lily patch (cleared bank → lily pads → reed edge): sits on a pad, occasional hop + ripple.
4. Rabbit: warren mound (dug earth → burrow mouth → clover ring): ambles a small meadow zone, sits up, vanishes into burrow at night.
5. Hedgehog: leaf-hollow (leaf pile → log hollow → moss roof): trundles the hedgerow at dusk only.
6. Butterflies: flowerbed (bed → blooms → full color): a small cloud that drifts between flowers.
7. Duck: reed nook (cleared reeds → nesting ring → ramp): paddles the shallows, tips forward to feed.
8. Turtle: basking log (log placed → sun spot → pond ramp): slow shore walk, long basks.

## SAGA ROSTER (one-of-a-kind)
9. Heron: reed-shallows stand (4 stages): stands motionless in the shallows, rare slow strike at the water. The island's sentinel.
10. Deer: mossy glade at the wood's edge (4 stages): grazes, lifts her head when you pass.
11. Fox: hillside den (4 stages): shy; seen at dusk, sits at the den mouth.
12. Otter: cove rocks + slide (4 stages): swims figure-eights, uses the slide.
13. The whale: THE SEA BELL (5 stages, a small bell tower on the pier's end): once built, you can ring it; sometimes, far offshore, the whale surfaces and answers. The biggest saga in the app. (Functional build + creature merged; offshore = no islet-law violation.)

## MOTION FEASIBILITY (MVP-honest)
Koi: sprite-on-curve arcs under the water tile, flip + 2-frame wiggle. Birds: perch points + point-to-point flits. Ground: waypoint amble inside a small home zone + idle sits. Saga residents: mostly stationary-majestic, one rare signature move each (heron strike, deer head-lift, whale surfacing). 2-3 poses per creature via the locked KIE recipe. No pathfinding, no collision AI: home zones only.

## THE GOAL-CREATION FLOW
1. Name the goal, break it into sub-goals, write the obstacle line (WOOP, existing grammar).
2. Pick the resident: roster shown with earned creatures in color, unearned as dark silhouettes (ROAD grammar). Tier auto-suggested from goal size; user confirms.
3. Place the habitat: island with glowing candidate spots (planting grammar, GF7).
4. Each finished sub-goal: habitat stage-up, WITNESSED (walk to it, hold ~820ms, grove grammar). The nest visibly gets ready for someone the whole goal long.
5. Completion: THE ARRIVAL. The creature walks/flies/swims in. First meeting. The user NAMES it (open David-call: naming yes/no; pro = identity evidence + attachment, con = cheese risk).
6. Forever after: tap any creature → its plaque: the goal that brought it + the completion date. The ledger is alive and tappable ("Koi #7: Ship the portfolio site, finished Mar 3").

## LAWS CARRIED
- Abandoned goal = the habitat WAITS at its stage, quietly, forever resumable. No death, no leaving, no decay, no shame line.
- Creatures are never buyable (life earned, comfort bought). Store sells no animals, ever.
- Zero notifications. Nothing "misses you."
- Density: ventures capped by zone (each creature has a home zone; zones are finite, which is honest: a small island holds a village of animals, not a zoo). Koi pond absorbs unlimited quests.
- No red anywhere (koi palette trap named above).
