ROUND 19: THE GOAL ENGINE. Base: Round 18 Turn 3, the grove-true 11A/14A skin, unchanged as the shell. [If a Q1 direction from the 2a-2e recuts got locked, render the list in that direction instead of the turn-3 list.] Carried verdicts, permanent: the goal's NAME is the only name anywhere, creatures are never named; "AWAKE" does not exist, a finished goal is DONE and reads like "done 2 june · lives on the island". What changes this round is the SUBSTANCE inside the frames. The engine below is law; render it in ten frames.

THE ONE BANNED PATTERN, first: a row's live line or CTA must NEVER name a carve stage. "next → rough form" is dead. The stone is the picture; the words are always the user's real life: "next · call the clinic". Every next-step line names an action a person can physically do that day.

COPY REGISTER, absolute: plain, direct, warm. No em-dashes anywhere, ever (use commas, periods, middle dots). No poetic riddle-questions, no half-serious metaphor nudges, no percent signs, no countdowns. A date may appear as plain text ("by Oct 1"); it never ticks, never turns red, never counts up or down.

THE CARVE STRIP follows THE ROAD's grammar exactly: done stages dim, current stage lit, future stages dark silhouettes. (This resolves the lit-past question from round 18 toward grove consistency.)

THE ENGINE (what the menu now knows how to do):
1. Creation is a 30-second path: name · one first step · optional "by when" · creature · place the boulder. Depth arrives AFTER the boulder stands, as skippable invitations: the WHY (one line in the user's own words: "What changes for you when this is done?"), then the obstacle PAIR: the obstacle plus "when that happens, I will ___" chosen from 3 plain chips or free text.
2. Steps are linted at entry: if the user types an outcome ("get healthier") instead of an action, ONE small chip offers a rewrite ("Try: 3 gym sessions this week"). Never blocks, offered once.
3. If a goal looks too thin for its name, the app pushes back exactly once, plainly: "This goal only has two steps. Add the rest if there's more to it." One tap either way, never repeated.
4. The card's three numbers: THIS WEEK (steps done this week) · TO NEXT STAGE (steps until the next carve) · BY OCT 1 (the plain date, if set). No "days since" counter anywhere.
5. A goal can link a PRACTICE: a step like "Deep work · 7 of 20 sessions". The practice stays a grove tree; each tracked session ticks both the tree and this goal. On the planner timeline, a block whose practice feeds a goal wears the goal's small stone badge in its corner.
6. THE WEEKLY ONE: at the week close, one question across all goals: "Which goal step matters most next week?" The chosen step becomes a real scheduled block on a real day, wearing the goal badge.
7. A quiet goal, when opened (never a notification), may show four plain chips once: "Still want this?" · "Does the next step feel doable?" · "Is something else pulling you?" · "Does the payoff feel far away?" Each routes differently: retire with dignity / shrink the step to its easy version / name the new obstacle / add a nearer step.
8. Two honest quiet states: PAUSED (the user chose it, the statue waits with full respect) and DRIFTING (nobody chose it: the row sits dim, no red, no counter, with a one-tap resolve: "Still on it" / "Set it down for now").
9. The middle stretch gets one honest line when work is logged but life hasn't visibly changed yet: "No visible change yet. That's the normal gap between doing the work and seeing it."
10. Only 2 or 3 big goals carve at once. Adding another asks, on one honest screen: "Which statue rests while this one carves?" Small errand-goals (koi) never count against this.

THE TEN FRAMES:
R1 THE LIST, engine-true: three rows with statue art and REAL action lines ("next · call the clinic"), one dim drifting row, one DONE row ("done 2 june · lives on the island"), and a quiet "wishes" entry at the bottom holding ideas that are not yet goals.
R2 THE CARD: carve strip (ROAD grammar) · the WHY line under the goal name · the three numbers · the obstacle line in quotes (tapping it reveals the "when that happens, I will" plan beneath) · the steps list including one practice-linked step ("Deep work · 7 of 20") · striped CTA with the next action.
R3 THE FIRST CUT, 30-second path: name, first step with the lint moment shown (user typed "get healthier", the chip offers "3 gym sessions this week"), optional "by Oct 1", creature, place the boulder.
R4 AFTER THE BOULDER: the invitation card: the WHY prompt, then the obstacle plus its plan chips.
R5 THE STALL: an opened quiet goal showing the four plain chips, and one route played out: the step shrinking to its easy version.
R6 PAUSED vs DRIFTING: the two states side by side with the one-tap resolve.
R7 THE PRACTICE LINK: the goal card's linked step, and beside it a planner timeline snippet where the 09:00 Deep work block wears the goal's stone badge.
R8 THE WEEKLY ONE: the week-close card asking the one question, the pick, and the confirmed block sitting on Tuesday.
R9 THE COMPLETION: the statue finishing (color rising from the feet, the creature stepping onto the grass), labels reading DONE ("done 11 aug · lives on the island"), with one optional line beneath: "What did that prove?"
R10 THE CAP: adding a fourth big goal: "Which statue rests while this one carves?" with the three current statues shown honestly.

LAWS carried: no red, no shame lines, no percent bars, no timers, forward-only copy, Baloo 2 800 titles, Tabler chrome glyphs only, the uploaded sprites are the only object art and are never redrawn, every card ends in an action, corners always tappable, the island photograph pixel-exact under every sheet.
