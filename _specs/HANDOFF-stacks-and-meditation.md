## 0. The one idea
A **self-help session = a STACK of tools run in order** (breathing → … → self-hypnosis / reprogramming — the layered order we already use). The app lets you **compose that stack like you Plan a Day** (recommended order you can reorder + add to, a length per item, a total session budget), then **walks you through it** one step at a time. And the **meditation** step inside it becomes far more nuanced — one epic length-adaptive guided meditation the app recommends, plus a menu of specific meditation types.

Everything stays true to the soul ([[alter-soul-clarified]]): adaptive, never forced, **always a way out**, gamified, de-cheesed.

## 1. The Stack Builder (compose a session — "Plan a Day" for your inner work)
- **Total budget first.** "How long do you have?" — 2 min · 10 · 20 · an hour. You get that budget to fill.
- **Recommended order, editable.** The app proposes a stack in the proven order (Steady the body → Clear the mind → Feel it through → Become who you're being → Lift the lens — the existing tool layers / the reprogramming stack: settle → install → reinforce). You can **add to it, remove, or reorder** (drag), exactly like the plan-a-day `orderStep`/`shapeFlow` builder — REUSE that system, don't reinvent.
- **Length per item.** Each tool in the stack gets a duration (chips like the day-planner: 2/5/10…), and the app fits them to the total budget (auto-scale or warn if over).
- **The order = our layered principle** (regulate before reprogram; you can't reframe a dysregulated body). The recommend engine encodes that dependency.

## 2. Working through the stack
- Once built, the app **runs the session in order** and **always suggests the next thing** (the Sequencer's "one next step," pointed at the stack). Never a wall — you see the current step + a gentle "next."
- Reuses the cockpit 'tool' stage: each step runs in the ring-tracked stage; finishing one advances to the next.
- Reward-never-shame; you can stop/shorten/skip any step at any time (the way-out at every turn).

## 4b. THE AUDIO COMPOSITION ENGINE (David 2026-07-01 — the key new idea)
Do **NOT** TTS a whole dynamic script at runtime. Instead the app **composes** each guided session by **stitching pre-recorded clips + programmatic silences** — so every word is the same calm male voice, and length/frequency are just scheduling parameters.

**The model:**
- **Clip bank** — a library of pre-recorded phrase clips (the existing `assets/voice/<hash>.mp3` pipeline), organised into **sections/pools** (e.g. arrival · breath · body-scan · awareness · closing), each pool holding several interchangeable lines synthesised from David's favourite teachers (the "one epic meditation, remixed in our voice").
- **Composer** — given the user's settings `{length, remindFreq, type}`, it lays out a schedule: pick section clips **in the right order**, then insert **silences between them sized so total ≈ chosen length** and **cue density ≈ chosen reminder frequency**. Longer session → longer gaps (more silence to sit in); "often" → shorter gaps, more re-anchors. "The app builds your custom audio from your settings by combining multiple tracks, in whatever order, with however many breaks."
- **Player** — plays clip → waits (clipDuration + gap) → next clip, on the primed `vaudio` channel. When the ordered clips run out on a long sit, it rests on the closing pool with longer gaps so it still feels guided.

**Why this shape:** guarantees voice consistency (no runtime TTS for fixed content), makes length/frequency truly adaptive (they're just gap math), and reuses the mp3 pipeline we already have. Custom/user-typed lines are the ONLY thing that might need on-device neural TTS later (#3 Piper) — and even those can be assembled from a bank where possible.

**Build order for the engine:** (1) record the section phrase-pools via `gen-voice.py` (extend it with a `POOLS` dict). (2) write `composeSession({length,freq,type})` → returns `[{clip, gapMs}]`. (3) rebuild `meditation()`'s run-loop to play that schedule instead of the fixed `setInterval(FREQ)`. (4) generalise the same composer to power a stack step. **Hard dependency: the on-device audio pipeline (v736 priming fix) must be confirmed working on David's iPhone FIRST — the whole engine rides `vaudio`.**

**Meditation "type" menu (RESOLVED 2026-07-01):** basic — 2-min sounds · 2-min body-scan; nuanced — heart · Vipassana. ("ridk" in the earlier note was just David typing "or idk" — NOT a type. No mystery tradition to chase.) Start with these + the epic adaptive default; the menu is extensible later.
