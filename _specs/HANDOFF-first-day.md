## 1. THE GAUGE-ROUTED OPEN (the core)
Every open — and especially the first — starts with the guardian reading state: "how's this week been?" → "and today?" (two taps, reuse MOODS).
- **LOW → the RELIEF door**: "got 5 minutes? I can make this better." → the micro-stack ritual (Ritual Engine R0). Tracker visibly turns on first ("I'm keeping this — everything real counts" — teaching tracking by doing). Ends with "another 5?" escalation. Structure is INVISIBLE inside it: the session is a tracked activity, the seed grows, the journey advances silently. A depleted user is never asked to be productive.
- **OKAY/GOOD → the STRUCTURE door**: the Five Stones (below).
The shipped 6-beat onboarding (showman → pact → energy+room → floor-task → coin → seed) SURVIVES as the pre-door: its energy answer IS the router's first data point.

## 2. THE FIVE STONES (the structure door's first day — jpNodes intercepted)
Hand-authored stones replace the adaptive trail until each completes; unlock in order across the real day. Sage narrates one line per stone (tutorial-as-whisper, retires per-surface after first use).
1. **DO** — "What are you doing right now?" → track one real thing, 1-5 min commit → claim-glow + spark + THE SEED SPROUTS A LEAF (visible cause-effect per stone).
2. **TELL** — one word: who do you want to be today? + one thing that would make today a win. Sage reflects it back ("A calm one. Then today bends around that."). Silently writes the AM bookend — never named "bookend."
3. **PROPOSE** — the app MAKES AN OFFER built from everything so far (energy + room + word + one-thing): "Running on empty + you want calm → just these two: [one thing] + [10 min bedroom]. Sound right?" Accept / swap / shrink. Count scales by energy (empty=1, good=3). For the working-not-planning persona (girlfriend), it proposes BREAK RITUALS, not task lists. "More control" door → full shapeFlow. (This is the pick-3-in-front-of-shapeFlow compromise David chose.)
4. **SEE** — guided swipe to the world; the seed has leaves matching completed stones; one line: "It only grows from real things. I can't water it. Only you can."
5. **CLOSE** — glows after ~5pm: "One honest line." Writes the PM bookend. Finale = Sage gives the day back: "Day one. Here's what I learned about you: [the real answers]. Tomorrow I use all of it." (First chronicle entry, spoken.)
Then the trail hatches into the normal adaptive engine — same voice, no ceremony of handover.
