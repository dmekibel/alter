## SECTION 1 — THE LAWS (what constrains any tool-system redesign)

**Soul principles (every feature obeys all 14):**

1. CONDENSE, DON'T PILE. The app possesses all tools and hands you ONE. A menu of options is the failure mode.
2. SYNTHESIZE, DON'T COPY. Every tool must be a fusion across sources, not a flat catalog. Copying a tool faithfully without remixing is the "soulless swarm" anti-pattern.
3. NUANCE = DEPTH WITHIN ONE PATH. Never add more options/tiles/questions to seem sophisticated. Make the existing path cleverer and more adaptive.
4. THE APP CARRIES THE LOAD. It reads the moment and hands the one right thing. Never pushes choosing back onto the user.
5. OFFLINE-CLEVER IS THE FLOOR. The verbatim KB-sourced scripts ship in app.js. The BYO AI key lubricates but never IS the product.
6. ACTIVE, NOT PEN-ON-PAPER. Tools change STATE through the body. A text field is the generic trap.
7. BODY-FIRST IS A HARD ORDERING LAW UNDER LOAD. Acute spike → sub-60s body reset FIRST, then label "That's Part X — not you," then the cognitive tool. Below-the-line allows L4 emotional-clearing (Stutz Active Love); only L5/L6 identity/perception tools are withheld until regulated.
8. IDENTITY-FIRST + INTRINSIC-ONLY. The morning installs WHO before WHAT. Why-chips offer only autonomy/identity reasons.
9. REWARD-NEVER-SHAME — a hard copy AND visual lint. Drift = dim-grey. Stripes/gold/shine = reward signals ONLY. Ban should/must/wasted/failed/missed. "Part X is NOT you."
10. NO NEON, NO GLOW-EVERYWHERE. The now-line is the single brightest thing. Reward shine is earned and rare. No gradients on drift, no gold-for-done, no white/see-through surfaces.
11. FIND YOUR WAY. Guidance has an off-switch. Universal tools, individual path.
12. THE GUARDIAN IS A WITNESS, NOT A COACH. Reflects and asks; never commands, scolds, or counts what you wasted. Must INITIATE yet never coerce.
13. ONE PLACE THE APP SPEAKS — the cockpit. Every guided flow renders in the cockpit stage. No third clashing menu.
14. FIRST PRINCIPLES, ON PAPER, ONE LEG AT A TIME. Concrete enumeration, not abstract philosophy.

**Profound layer — the five deep differentiators (every mechanic must serve one or be cut):**

- CATCH = UNIT OF PROGRESS. The distraction-tap in meditation is the thesis of the whole app. Noticing IS the rep. Applied fractal-wide: return after a gap earns a flagship T5 card; bounce-back speed is the learnable metric (average gap shrinking), not perfect adherence. Replan is a skill you level. Skipped tools are a compass, not a shame signal.
- GRADUATION = ENDGAME. Skills internalize: declining reminder-density (medCadence generalized) → Grace tier stable 2 weeks → graduation prompt. The win condition is stopping needing catching. The app openly optimizes for its own disappearance.
- IDENTITY EVIDENCE, NOT REPS. The tracker logs court evidence. At doubt moments the guardian cites the record: "You've come back from drift 9 times. The data says you're someone who returns."
- TIME-SYMMETRY. PM→AM letters across time: the app delivers the morning's own line back at night. The compounding view: what your current average week, repeated a year, actually builds — from real data, no fantasy.
- ALIGNMENT AS THE ONE NUMBER. Plan (identity claim + ONE thing) vs. Track (what actually happened). Private, never punitive; determines routing (high = offer growth tools; low = route to replan + recovery). Catches close the alignment gap; the world blooms in proportion to closure.
