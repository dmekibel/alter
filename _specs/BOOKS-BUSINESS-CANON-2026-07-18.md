## PART 1: CONVERGENT LAWS (3+ books agree; treat as near-law)

1. **Price from evidence, never from guesses or competitors.** (Monetizing, Pricing Man, Mom Test, Virtual Economies.) WTP conversations BEFORE locking prices; ask about past behavior and trade-offs, never "would you pay X"; the behavior ladder (what people DO) outranks every stated answer. Copying Calm's price = "outsourcing your price decision."
2. **Never start low. Price signals quality; premium is a position you defend.** (Monetizing, Pricing Man, Obviously Awesome.) Easy to lower a high price; nearly impossible to raise a low one (Mercedes could only correct 3-5%/yr). ALTER = premium checklist, not volume.
3. **A fast sellout is an underpricing SIGNAL, not a win.** (Monetizing "minivation", Pricing Man Audi Q7/Playmobil.) If $49 evaporates in hours with zero resistance: advance the ladder faster; never reopen the tier.
4. **Scarcity must be real, QUANTITY-framed, hard-dated.** (Cialdini: quantity beats time-framing; Launch: ambiguous close halves conversion; Virtual Economies: positional goods; Hooked: honest Worchel scarcity.) "Осталось 23 из 30" beats "до пятницы". Founding = never reissued, ever (Habbo reissue-devaluation case).
5. **The launch is an EVENT with a sequence, not an announcement.** (Launch PLF, Cialdini, Breakthrough gradualization.) Pre-survey → 3 content pieces (no sale until piece 3) → 5-day open cart → close-day double-push (~50% of sales land on close day).
6. **Only costly commitment counts as signal.** (Mom Test currencies: time/reputation/cash; Cialdini active-public-effortful-free; Hooked investment; Virtual Economies behavior ladder.) Compliments = zero data. The Stripe pre-order IS the test.
7. **Give first, unconditionally.** (Cialdini $5-check-beats-$50-promise; Launch prelaunch value; Rework out-teach; Hooked reward-before-investment.) Free teaching content and a real free core are the engine of reciprocity and audience ownership.
8. **In a saturated category, the MECHANISM is the headline.** (Breakthrough sophistication stage 3-4; Obviously Awesome unique attributes; matches our Foundation Pitch.) "Одно вместо шести" and "никогда не наказывает" are mechanisms; lead with them, never with bigger promises.
9. **Never discount the core; add value instead.** (Pricing Man goods-beat-discounts + premium law; Monetizing 3-non-price-alternatives rule; Virtual Economies time-limited windows only; Obviously Awesome: a markdown says "it was never worth $99".) Black Friday = gift/bonus event, January = fresh-start event; the lifetime price tag itself does not move.
10. **The owned list is THE strategy.** (Launch: responsiveness beats size, buyer worth 10-15x prospect; Rework: audience before you need it.) Email capture from day one; social is rented land.
11. **One dominant lever per wave; stacking all levers reads as manipulation.** (Cialdini/Friestad-Wright; Launch trigger sequencing.) Founding = scarcity+commitment. BF = reciprocity/gift. January = fresh-start commitment + founder social proof.
12. **Sell what it DOES, benefits not features.** (Breakthrough functional-product law; Monetizing benefit statements; Obviously Awesome feature→benefit→value.) "Delete five apps" is a benefit spine; a six-item feature dump is Feature Shock.

No peer-to-peer transfer, no cash-out, no resale: sidesteps fraud/regulatory swamp and is easy to explain to mom ("не крипта, не казино"). Anti-MUDflation: hold reward supply per-capita stable; add new SINKS (new things to spend on) rather than more faucets. No $850 whale harpoons: cap per-item prices at honest levels.
9. **Subscription = a later TEST on colder cohorts, not a launch decision.** (Pricing Man fitness-studio retention argument FOR monthly; Launch/Rework lifetime-first.) Sequence: founding lifetime (Aug) → doors (autumn) → test "всё сразу" monthly on January cohort. This is the три-двери sequencing, now book-backed.
10. **Review strategy: never curate to 5.0.** (Cialdini: 4.2-4.7 optimal, visible negatives raise conversion 67%.) Ask honest reviews, answer negatives personally, never seed fakes.

## PART 4: MODEL VERDICT AFTER 10 BOOKS (the три двери, refined)

- **Door 1 (founding lifetime, then standard lifetime + one-off programs):** the launch engine. Premium position, upfront cash, zero churn ops, matches near-zero marginal cost. Books: Launch, Rework, Pricing Man, Monetizing skimming.
- **Door 2 (many small honest doors):** cosmetics + AI-companion credits + voice/program one-offs. Closed-loop tokens, nonlinear pack pricing (bigger pack = bonus credits, per Pricing Man incremental-discount law), infinite-variability content pipeline required (Hooked: finite cosmetic sets decay; the journal/adaptive content is our infinite engine). Higher-risk door; needs volume; never self-executes.
- **Door 3 (subscription "всё сразу"):** the retention-psychology counterweight (paying monthly re-activates "use it or lose it"). Test in January on cold cohort; priced from WTP data, not from Calm.
- **The free core is not a door; it is the ground.** It buys trust, category position, reciprocity, and the audience. (Finch + Bible App + Rework out-teach all point here.)
