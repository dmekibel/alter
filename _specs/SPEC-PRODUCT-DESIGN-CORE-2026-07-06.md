## 1. The core swap: a rep, not a coin
June's Journey's daily hook is "collect coins." A coin accumulates **quantity** — a bigger pile of the same nothing. ALTER's daily deposit is **a rep of a self-help tool**, which accumulates **power** — it changes *you*. Delete the app and you keep the coins? No. Delete the app and you keep the ability to reverse desire in a hard moment? **Yes.** The rep is the only deposit that survives everything.

Quick is fine (one breath, one Reversal of Desire, one glimpse) — because the point isn't the effort, it's the **accumulation toward automaticity.**

### Why quick reps compound (the four sources, one mechanism)
Repetition installs a tool until it fires *without you*:
- **Maltz (Psycho-Cybernetics):** the self-image is a servo-mechanism; reps re-aim it until the response is automatic (~21 days min).
- **Brian Johnson (Heroic, in `_course/`):** an "algorithm" = a trained if-then protocol practiced until it's identity, not effort. Every rep = a **+1**.
- **Clear (Atomic Habits):** a habit is a behavior made automatic through reps; each rep = *a vote for who you're becoming*; the 2-min rule legitimizes quick reps; make it **satisfying** (immediate reward).
- **Stutz (The Tools):** the tools are quick somatic protocols that *only work through repetition* — and are already visual (→ the sigil).

## 2. The sigil = the compression of a practiced tool into a one-glance trigger
David's keystone idea. A **sigil charged with meaning** is a conditioned anchor (classical conditioning / Maltz's mental image / an NLP-style anchor). Do the tool → charge its sigil; over reps, seeing the sigil *fires the whole tool instantly*. That's why it works even done quickly: at high charge, **the glance *is* the rep.**

In ALTER's pixel language, each tool gets a glyph that **charges like the timeline battery**: dim → glowing → **installed** (automatic). The full method (Spare) is mined at `_mined/spiritual-canon/sigil-mechanic.md`; the *performance* of the charge is the Ritual Engine.

**The collection is therefore a constellation of charged sigils = the tools you've actually installed in yourself.** Pride in real power — the honest inversion of the June's Journey island (see that spec: *her island is a beautiful record of nothing; ALTER's is a beautiful record of a real life lived*).

## 3. Goals-within-goals — WITHOUT gatekeeping (the key resolution)
June's Journey's interlocking goals ("do the minigame → get the token → unlock the thing") create investment by gating **access**. David explicitly rejects gatekeeping — *all tools available to everyone.* The resolution is a hard line:

> **Gate DEPTH, never ACCESS.** Every tool is always available; reps unlock *depth, combinations, and revelation* — never permission. Nothing is ever behind a wall; new things *emerge* from what you've practiced.

So "do meditation 3× to unlock X" is reframed: you can *always* meditate. Doing it 3× **installs** the meditation sigil, which then **combines** with your other charged tools into something that genuinely couldn't exist before you practiced the parts. Not a gate — an **emergence** (a synthesis honestly requires its parts).

### The three-tier structure (all emergent, nothing locked)
June's Journey's chain (scene→coins→decorate→flowers→chapter) rebuilt so every link is real growth:
1. **The rep** (daily deposit) — run a tool, charge its sigil. Always free, always available. *(the daily-coin replacement)*
2. **The stack / "algorithm"** — charged sigils **combine** into a personal protocol for a real situation ("my anxiety-reset stack" — a Johnson-style if-then). This is the existing **Sequencer / stacks** design ([[wisdom-mining-done]], the stacks-and-meditation spec), deepened. The goal-within-a-goal: install the tools that fuse into *your* algorithm.
3. **The journey / identity arc** — completed stacks advance your arc + the guardian's chronicle of who you're becoming. The goal-within-the-goal-within-the-goal: *becoming the person* (Atomic Habits' identity engine).

### The honest-vs-dark guardrail (the test)
Interlocking is honest **only when every step is intrinsically valuable** and the goal-within-a-goal is a **real synthesis**. Test every nested goal: *"if she did just this one step and stopped, did she still gain something real?"* Yes → honest. Only-valuable-as-a-means-to-the-next-hoop → it's the June's Journey dark half (arbitrary busywork engineered to pad time / hide monetization) — **cut it.**

## 4. The six micro-mechanics (the honest versions of the category's hooks)
*(Captured here so they're not lost — they underpin the whole daily loop.)*
1. **Give before asking.** Every winner grants reward *then* asks (Habit Rabbit gifts 100 carrots, then profiles). Reciprocity: each first-run ask should be *preceded* by the guardian giving something (a blessing, a charge, a named observation).
2. **The mascot has an opinion about *now*.** The companion reacts to time-of-day/context (nightcap at bedtime), not just to your actions. The guardian should already know it's 11pm and look like it. Presence, cheap to build.
3. **Select, don't compose.** Generation is the dropout point. Every input is a selection that can be amended, never a blank composition.
4. **One physical, singular progress object.** Not a dashboard — *one object that accumulates* (jar/halo/constellation), referenced by name in the guardian's speech.
5. **The companion owns tomorrow.** End each session mid-gesture — something visibly half-finished only tomorrow completes (a cliffhanger held by the guardian, not a nagging notification).
6. **Every ask is bounded.** "Step 2 of 3, ~2 min." Never an unbounded ritual.

## 5. Honest monetization — "free buys the transformation, paid buys the expression & depth"
David **will** charge (model TBD, [[alter-timeless-tools-and-monetization]]). The rule that respects non-payers absolutely and still makes everyone-who-can-pay *want* to:

> **Never charge for growth or access. Charge for beauty, bespoke-ness, and more content.** A free user becomes a genuinely better person, full stop.

Three honest paid surfaces, strongest first:
1. **Customization / self-expression** — *the honest "decorate the island."* Customize your sigils, the guardian's look, the grimoire/constellation aesthetic, themes. Gates **nothing functional**; rides the collector-pride the free product already built (IKEA effect — pay to make what's *yours* beautiful). The incentive hits everyone who can pay because it's *downstream of pride they already feel.*
2. **Depth-of-content packs** — base tools free forever; pay for *more* (a Stutz deep-cut pack, a Dispenza series, seasonal wisdom drops, deeper grimoire chapters). **More, never unblocking.**
3. **The guardian that knows *you*** — free = tools + solid guidance; paid = the AI guardian that reads your patterns and composes bespoke algorithms and mirrors you deeply. Real value (a smarter guardian), not a wall.

**One-liner:** *Free makes you better. Paid makes you better with a grimoire that's beautifully, uniquely yours and a guardian that knows you intimately.*

### The refusal list (from the June's Journey honest/dark split — do NOT copy)
Energy/wait walls · loot boxes / gacha · dual currency (obscures cost) · FOMO countdowns on digital goods · guilt-dismiss copy ("maybe later / show again") · whale price-discrimination · **loss-aversion on the collection itself** ("lose it if you stop" — the line between pride and anxiety). The separating rule: *June's Journey needs you to keep depositing into an account IT controls and can devalue; ALTER's deposits must belong to YOU — meaningful, exportable, true even if you quit tomorrow.*
