## 1. THE GOLD — gamifiable ideas mapped to ALTER's core

Organized by the surface each idea deepens, because that is how they get built. Within each surface, strongest first. Everything here is buildable against an existing surface; the honest "no clean hook" ideas are quarantined at the end of the section so they don't masquerade as backlog.

### 1A. THE PM BOOKEND — the Shutdown Ritual is the missing spine

This is the highest-density cluster in the whole corpus. Four separate subjects independently converged on the same mechanic, which is the strongest possible signal that it is real.

**The Shutdown Ritual → the PM bookend gains a fixed 3-beat completion sequence.**
- *Essence:* a fixed end-of-day sequence — capture loose loops, stage tomorrow, say a completion phrase — that gives the brain explicit permission to stop.
- *Mechanism (as a feeling, not a fact):* uncaptured obligations keep firing as background tension that blocks rest; a trusted capture plus a spoken close tells the nervous system the day is actually over. Over ~a month of repetition, the rumination groove "fills in until it's shallow enough that your thoughts don't get stuck in it."
- *Protocol:* three fixed beats every night — (1) **capture** any open loops from today's timeline into the backlog, (2) **stage** tomorrow's first block so the morning has zero decisions, (3) **seal** with a spoken/typed completion phrase. Streak-tracked. A single yes/no the next morning: "did your mind let go last night?" — the metric that should trend to zero-rumination over weeks.
- *ALTER hook — precise:* the PM bookend already exists but is a reflection prompt, not a mechanical close. Give it the literal 3-beat sequence above, and make **beat 2 charge tomorrow's first block** — the SEAL of tonight is the PRIME of tomorrow. That single wire (PM SEAL → AM's first charged block) is the cleverest move in the whole document because it makes the two bookends one loop instead of two islands. The completion phrase is the journey grammar's SEAL applied to a day instead of a stone.

**Capture-everything pass → the PM bookend's beat 1 is a real sweep, not a vibe.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* the brain stays stressed while it's responsible for remembering; externalizing to a trusted place frees attention. "Your brain gets stressed when it's worried about forgetting things."
- *Protocol:* the PM ritual can't reach SEAL until every open loop from the day is either placed on a future timeline block or dropped to backlog. Nothing stays only in the user's head.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens PM beat 1 exactly — the guardian's dad-principle move ("I carry the load") made mechanical. The guardian holds the loops so David doesn't have to.

**Value + Service reflection → the PM bookend's reflection question gets teeth.**
- *Essence:* meaning = perceiving that your action created value for someone + served someone. "Meaning isn't found — it's built, through earning value and serving people."
- *Protocol:* replace generic gratitude with a two-part close: "what did today create, and who did it serve?" Flag days where both answers are blank for redesign.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the PM reflection line itself — a sharper question than gratitude, and it feeds identity-evidence with a meaning-signal, not just a mood.

### 1B. THE AM BOOKEND — the Morning Ritual as designed keystone

**Morning Ritual as keystone → the AM bookend becomes same-sequence-every-time with a hard end marker.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* "Your morning is not a test of how much discipline you have, it's a test of how well you've designed your morning." Ritualization bypasses willpower; the first 30–90 min post-wake is a plastic window where early wins carry the day.
- *Protocol:* track simple yes/no completion of the SAME sequence (not quality, not optimization). A **hard end-time** to the ritual is the trigger that starts the real day. Keystone streak = identity-evidence.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the AM bookend directly — lock the sequence, add the hard-end marker, track it as ONE keystone streak rather than a variable checklist. Newport's "design, don't willpower" is the same claim as ALTER's environment-over-motivation stance.

**Fixed-schedule "done by" time → AM commits it, PM checks it.**
- *Essence:* commit to a stop time in advance and work backward; the boundary forces triage (Parkinson's Law used deliberately).
- *Protocol:* the AM bookend's intention-set includes choosing today's "done by" time; the PM bookend checks against it as the first honesty beat.
- *ALTER hook:* closes the loop between the two bookends a second way — morning commitment, evening honesty. Pairs perfectly with the PM shutdown.

**Energy-aware "scale today" → wire the existing mood gauge into a plan-shrink action.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* output ≈ energy × intentionality; on low-energy days the only lever left is intentionality. Ad-hoc drift on tired days compounds exhaustion and guilt.
- *Protocol:* a low-energy read at the AM bookend triggers an explicit "scale today" — pre-shrink to 2–3 items and an earlier stop, tracked as its OWN successful-day type, never a failure state.
- *ALTER hook:* ALTER's energy-door mood gauge already routes every open. This makes the low-energy branch DO something structural at the AM bookend instead of only softening tone. Directly matches never-hands-over: a light day is honored, not flagged as a drop-off.

### 1C. THE PLANNER / TIMELINE — convert ambient compulsions into finite blocks

The timeline's core law is already "unscheduled time gets a job." Newport's planning corpus is almost entirely fuel for that law.

**Time-blocking as reality check → the timeline gains an honest "does today fit" signal.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* a to-do list has infinite room; a calendar has finite room. Seeing that commitments literally don't fit is the "productivity dragon" confrontation no list gives. "Give every minute a job."
- *Protocol:* distinguish blocks-with-a-named-job from empty/unclaimed time; when placed blocks overflow the available hours, show an overflow signal rather than silently letting them collide. The AM bookend includes a give-every-minute-a-job pass.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the timeline into an honest capacity mirror, not just a scheduler. This is high-leverage and very ALTER-native (honest craft, no dark patterns) — the timeline stops flattering the user.

**Batch, don't stream + Newspaper mode → checking becomes a placed, bounded block.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* continuous checking costs 5–20 min recovery per switch and rides tribal "someone tapped my shoulder" wiring; algorithmic feeds optimize for arousal. Batching short-circuits both.
- *Protocol:* "check messages" and "read the news" become actual scheduled timeline blocks (e.g. 2×/day), not ambient always-available actions. Track checked-in-window vs impulsive-check; log a declining "urges outside window" count.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the timeline the way the core law already wants — an ambient compulsion converted into a finite, placed, sealed block. One build covers both ideas (messages + news are the same mechanic).

**Autopilot scheduling → a recurring block type that never needs re-deciding.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* repeated micro-decisions are a hidden tax; fixing the slot converts a decision into a habit. "Tuesday at 12, I go to this library — you don't even have to think about it."
- *Protocol:* tag a block "autopilot" once; it auto-places every week. Show a "decisions saved" count, not a task count.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the timeline with a distinct block type so recurring rituals (bookends, tool sessions) stop needing re-placement, and the timeline visually separates locked-in rhythm from negotiable time. Rituals are exactly what should be autopiloted.

**The Doubling / 50% rule → a calibration nudge learned from the user's own history.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* humans systematically under-estimate task time; a blunt 2× (or +50%) correction fixes the overstuffed-plan failure loop, and the buffer shrinks as real data accumulates.
- *Protocol:* log estimated vs actual on completed timed blocks; if actual routinely overruns, auto-suggest an inflation factor next time.
- *ALTER hook:* touches both the timeline and the charging primitive — a "this usually runs long, want to charge more?" nudge learned from David's own logs. Turns invested time into an honest number. Do NOT over-build this; it's a small nudge, not a forecasting engine.

**Quick-capture → a zero-friction affordance feeding the backlog.**
- *Essence:* capture the moment an obligation appears; don't hold it in your head, and don't force the multi-step block editor.
- *Protocol:* one tap, no categorization, dumps to an inbox/backlog; the PM shutdown sweeps it.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the planner with a capture path distinct from full block creation. Pairs with the PM shutdown (which is where captures get processed). Small, high-use.

**Re-plan after disruption → a blown day triggers a gentle re-plan, not a broken streak.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* plans are best guesses, not commands; the response to disruption is to promptly re-plan the remainder, not to feel guilt. "You get gold stars for doing your work well, not for sticking to a plan."
- *Protocol:* after a missed/blown-up day, prompt a lightweight "re-plan the rest of today/week"; credit the re-plan itself as the tracked good behavior.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens how the timeline handles missed blocks AND the identity-evidence framing of streaks. This IS the catch-is-the-unit thesis in planning form: the re-plan is the rep, the disruption was never the miss. Extremely ALTER-native.

**Context/role batching → a per-block role tag + a fragmentation read.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* switching mental models between roles burns energy; staying in one context lets the mind settle into flow in 10–15 min.
- *Protocol:* tag blocks by role/life-domain; visually cluster same-context blocks; surface a "context switches today" count to minimize.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the day/week view with a role tag and a simple clustered-vs-fragmented read. Also gives the planner a domain-segregated filter so the day isn't a "wall of everything." Modest but clean.

**Overload awareness / soft cap → the plate meter.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* each commitment carries fixed overhead regardless of size; past a threshold, overhead consumes the schedule (the overhead spiral). Overload is structural, not a discipline failure.
- *Protocol:* a visible commitment-count / "plate" meter that WARNS (never shames) when concurrent open threads exceed a sane number, nudging consolidation before adding a new one.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the planner with an overload signal AND caps how many journey stones can be "active" at once (see 1E). Prevents ALTER itself from becoming a source of overload — an anti-engagement move, on-brand for the moat.

### 1D. THE CHARGING PRIMITIVE — deterministic investment, made honest and progressive

The charging primitive is already ALTER's felt-investment seed. Newport gives it three upgrades and one design-integrity law.

**Cognitive debt / struggle-is-the-feature → the design law that protects the charge from shortcuts.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* effortful Type-2 work is literally the training stimulus, the way muscular strain builds strength; removing the strain removes the growth. "The hardness is the feature."
- *Protocol / law:* the hold-to-charge already IS "struggle made physical." Guardrail: never add a skip button, an auto-complete, or a variable jackpot to the charge. The effort must be felt.
- *ALTER hook:* this is language and a guardrail for the primitive's core thesis, not a feature. It tells David never to shortcut the charge. Pairs with the anti-slot-machine law below.

**Anti-slot-machine / clean reward → charge growth is deterministic and effort-proportional, and says so.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* variable-ratio reward is more compulsive but corrosive; sustainable motivation needs consistent, self-caused reward tied to real effort ("I caused this," not "I got lucky").
- *Protocol:* same effort → same payout curve, always. No surprise jackpots. Make it explicit in the copy.
- *ALTER hook:* a design-integrity principle positioning the charge as the exact opposite of the apps David is countering. This is the moat (honest craft) expressed in a single mechanic's math.

**Progressive charge ladder → hold-duration grows week over week on a scheduled slot.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* cognitive stamina is trainable via graduated exposure (10→15→20→40 min), the way physical stamina is; schedule it like a workout instead of waiting for mood.
- *Protocol:* the charge-hold for a given practice has a duration ladder tied to a fixed weekly slot, growing visibly over weeks.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the primitive from an in-the-moment gesture into a progressive, scheduled ladder — reps first, and the ladder itself is identity-evidence.

**Compounding visualization → the "slow and steady" proof over months.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* consistent modest daily deposits compound non-linearly; tiny consistent charges beat rare huge ones. "Hard wood grows slowly." Festina lente — slow but relentless.
- *Protocol:* the charge-history view shows session-frequency-over-time on a single thread (a consistency graph), celebrating showing-up over burst intensity, with a months-horizon compounding visual.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the charge-history surface to make "slow but relentless" the felt shape of investment. Reinforces the primitive's whole thesis. Combine the ladder + compounding + consistency-graph into ONE charge-history redesign, not three widgets.

### 1E. THE JOURNEY STONES — the missing "quarter" layer, and the PRIME upgrade

The journey is a Duolingo ladder of PRIME→DO→NAME→PLACE→SEAL stones. Newport's planning cascade and ambition work give the journey its missing time-scale and a much stronger PRIME.

**Episodic Future Thinking → PRIME becomes a vivid future-self vignette, not an aphorism.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* the brain has two motivation systems — fast dopamine, and Episodic Future Thinking (vivid imagined futures tied to identity). EFT is the only thing strong enough to beat engineered dopamine. "If you really connect who you are today with a future you viscerally want to wake up in, your brain will choose the hard path."
- *Protocol:* pair each stone/habit with an explicit "which future does this serve" tag; the PRIME step is a concrete sensory future-self snapshot that primes THIS specific rep.
- *ALTER hook:* the single best fit in the corpus. It deepens both the PRIME step (Tony-style priming, already the journey law) and identity-evidence: evidence becomes proof accumulating toward a NAMED future, not generic streaks. This is Dispenza's "rehearse the future self" and Robbins' priming — both already canon roster thinkers — arriving from Newport's angle. Build here first on the journey side.

**Multi-scale planning cascade → the active stone seeds the week, day blocks trace back to the stone.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* each scale answers a question the others can't — quarter gives direction, week bridges to calendar, day converts intention to action. Skip a level → aimless grinding or ungrounded fantasy.
- *Protocol:* the active stone visibly seeds the week's planner focus; a day block can show which stone it serves; a weekly review derives the week from the stone.
- *ALTER hook:* the journey stones ARE the missing quarter/vision layer that the planner currently lacks. Wire a visible thread: today's block → this week's focus → this quarter's stone. This is the connective tissue between the journey and the planner — two surfaces that currently don't talk. High-leverage.

**Pragmatic-ambition savor gate → advancing to the next stone requires a savor window.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* grand ambition = permanent want-overload (always someone further ahead); pragmatic ambition is bounded (~12 months) and its payoff compounds daily once achieved. The deliberate savor phase is what stops it sliding back into endless chasing. "When you accomplish a pragmatic ambition you have to put in effort to enjoy the fruits."
- *Protocol:* completing a stone unlocks a mandatory ~90-day "savor" period before the next rung; the PLACE/SEAL step names the daily benefit now in hand.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the stone ladder so it's a pragmatic-ambition ladder, not an endless unlock-chain. The SEAL explicitly names the ongoing benefit. Directly serves never-hands-over: the reward is enjoyed, not immediately traded up. Very ALTER-native, anti-engagement.

**Selection layer / WIP cap → advancing requires retiring something; cap active stones at 2–3.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* the productivity funnel is Selection → Organization → Execution, and everyone ignores the top (selection). Overload traces to never saying no upstream. Also: "ideas are easy, execution is hard" — cap active threads, ship before starting new.
- *Protocol:* advancing to a new stone requires consciously retiring/de-prioritizing an old one; active stones capped at 2–3; a visible "parked, not started" count.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens stone-unlock logic so growth is a trade-off, not free accumulation — the funnel's selection layer made visible and gamified. Reinforces graduation = guidance maturing.

**Reverse goal-setting → the PRIME for a big stone is a backward-chain builder.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* for genuinely transformative goals, start from the end-state and work backward through milestones to reveal the true prerequisite chain, then commit to a concrete first 30 days.
- *Protocol:* on a big/transformative goal, the stone's PRIME is a guided reverse-chain (end-state → 3–5 milestones in reverse → concrete first-30-days), which becomes the DO content.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the PRIME→DO grammar specifically for ambitious stones — a guided exercise instead of an aphorism. Use only on the rare big stone, not every stone.

**Deliberate practice / proof-project → stone completion requires a real, staked DO.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* abstract practice fails without accountability; a bounded, publicly-committed project with a deadline and a "witness" simulates coaching pressure. Also: skill grows from narrow stretch + tight feedback, not volume.
- *Protocol:* stone completion criteria require a concrete, time-boxed proof-project (with a named witness / visible log), not passive lesson-consumption. The DO step carries a real external stake.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens stone completion so PRIME→DO→NAME→PLACE→SEAL has real stakes at DO. Also gives guided "training" tools a stretch+feedback logging structure (log the rep AND the feedback signal, not just time) — matches the just-shipped depth-build pattern of real protocols over aphorisms.

**Career-capital "passion is earned" → a dedicated stone that kills the passion-search myth.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* passion is a byproduct of building rare, valuable skills — skill → autonomy → passion, not passion → skill. "The passion is cultivated, the passion is grown."
- *Protocol:* PRIME kills the passion-search myth; DO is a commitment ritual (pick one skill-in-progress, deepen it for a fixed period, log growing competence); NAME/SEAL land it as identity-evidence (skill built, not passion found).
- *ALTER hook:* a clean, high-value single stone. Career capital is relative, not elite ("rare within your marketplace") — so the reframe is accessible to every persona, not just ambitious ones.

**Seasonality → the journey/streak honors intentional light seasons.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* humans evolved for cyclical intensity, not flat output; without designed seasonality, pseudo-productivity and burnout follow. "We evolved to have heavier periods and lighter periods."
- *Protocol:* mark stretches Push / Steady / Recovery in advance; during Recovery the journey/planner relaxes intensity targets and CELEBRATES lighter engagement instead of flagging a drop-off.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens both identity-evidence and the journey path — streaks recognize intentional lighter seasons as success. This is never-hands-over and guidance-matures made literal. Pairs with the intensity dial (1F).

### 1F. IDENTITY-EVIDENCE — the reframe layer, and the structure behind the log

Identity-evidence is ALTER's closest existing analog to Newport's whole "discipline is an identity" line. These sharpen it.

**Discipline as identity → the copy/framing law for every logged action.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* discipline is a practiced self-image built from evidence, not an innate trait. Small consistent proof-points in unglamorous areas shift self-conception. "Discipline is really more an identity that you develop than an approach to a particular challenge."
- *Protocol:* every completed block/ritual logs as identity-evidence ("you showed up as a disciplined person"), not neutral task-completion. A visible, growing discipline log framed explicitly as evidence for a self-image.
- *ALTER hook:* this is nearly a verbatim statement of ALTER's core mechanic (Maltz's self-image-as-master-program, already canon). It's a framing/copy sharpening, not a new feature: make the evidence log say "evidence you are becoming X," never "task done." Directly serves thesis truth 3.

**Lead indicators, not lag → goal capture decomposes vague goals into controllable daily metrics.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* lag indicators (the end-state) give no instruction for today; lead indicators are inputs you fully control, so tracking them daily builds real momentum and removes the anxiety of judging against an unreachable outcome.
- *Protocol:* when a user sets any goal/stone target, force-decompose it into ONE trackable daily/weekly lead metric (a count, a duration, a rep); store and display that, not the vague aspiration.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens goal-capture across the planner and journey — ALTER scaffolds the vague wish into a lead metric instead of storing the wish verbatim. High-leverage because it improves everything downstream.

**Non-linear reward / focus-tier → each identity-thread is tagged Major Focus vs Baseline.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* effort→reward is non-linear; splitting attention keeps every thread on the flat part of the curve, while sustained focus on one produces a discontinuous jump. Baseline almost everything; pour into a few.
- *Protocol:* the user names ONE major-focus thread per season plus 1–2 baseline threads (fixed minimal maintenance); the app visually distinguishes focus-charging from baseline-maintaining and prompts a quarterly "rotate the focus" checkpoint once a reward-jump is logged.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the evidence log so it TEACHES non-linear investment instead of tracking flat parallel streaks. Pairs with the WIP cap and the charging primitive. This is "do fewer things" rendered as a data structure.

**A firm "no" as evidence → log an intentional no.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* people run ~20% overloaded to manufacture a stress-excuse for declining; most requests are already silently declined. A deliberate no reclaims margin. "Only a firm no truly reduced our commitments."
- *Protocol:* log "a no I said on purpose" as a trackable identity-evidence entry; a positive metric, never a failure.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the evidence model with a currently-untracked identity dimension (boundary-setting). Small, clever, cheap. Ties to the plate meter.

**Trust the quarter → a quarterly rollup as the headline metric, de-emphasizing daily streaks.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* short-timescale self-grading ("did I win today?") produces anxiety disproportionate to real output; slow productivity only becomes visible over a quarter. "Trust the quarter."
- *Protocol:* a quarterly "this season so far" rollup as the primary progress ritual; daily views deliberately de-emphasized for scoring.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the journey's PLACE/SEAL with a season rollup that counter-balances any daily-streak pressure elsewhere. An honest counterweight — anti-engagement.

**Values → living values doc = the user-blueprint, re-surfaced seasonally.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* perfect values clarity is impossible upfront; a rough written values doc, revisited at fixed checkpoints, beats none because it lets you move intentionally instead of drift.
- *Protocol:* the user writes a rough values doc early; the app re-surfaces it at seasonal/birthday checkpoints; decisions and stone-choices reference it.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens onboarding + identity-evidence — ALTER's user-blueprint/persona profile IS this living values document, re-surfaced rather than captured once and forgotten. Every tracked action ties back to a named value, not just a streak. This is the constitution-level frame (values-first vision → everything follows).

### 1G. GUIDED TOOLS — a few real additions, one honest new category

**Sit-with-boredom timer → a "train the boredom muscle" tool.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* boredom is a redirect signal toward meaningful action; numbing it kills the signal. Tolerance is trainable over 4–6 weeks. "Boredom is not the enemy; constant distraction is the enemy."
- *Protocol:* a duration-without-stimulation timer (like breathwork/PMR), explicitly named as training boredom-tolerance, feeding identity-evidence ("you sat with nothing for 10 minutes and didn't reach for the phone").
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the guided-tool set with one honest tool that is distinctly ALTER (anti-distraction, felt, no gamified stimulation). Distinct from meditation — the point is nothing, not guidance.

**Quiet-brain / solitude timer → a no-input reflection tool with a "what surfaced" log.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* solitude (no external input) is where experiences get fit into a coherent self-framework; without it you absorb others' values instead of discovering your own.
- *Protocol:* a 15–25 min zero-input block, paired with a short "what surfaced" prompt afterward, logged as its own practice type.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the AM bookend or the tool set — genuinely nothing (no audio, no guidance), tracked with a reflective note rather than task completion. Could be one tool with the boredom timer; don't build two near-identical timers.

**Creative-fatigue check-in → a "stuck" flag that prescribes the 48-hour-then-walk protocol.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* physical tiredness says stop; creative fatigue says the approach isn't working. The fix is a deliberate 48-hour step-back (subconscious restructuring), then a 20-min walk — not more grinding. "Without the 48 hours before that 20 minutes I would not have had the insight."
- *Protocol:* a "stuck" flag on a goal/project triggers a 48-hour cooldown suggestion + a follow-up walk nudge, instead of more reminders to grind.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens an existing reflective tool (journaling / reprogramming toolkit) with a creative-fatigue branch. Small, high-signal, very un-generic.

**Digital-pacification audit → name the need a habit scratches, pick a real replacement.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* distraction tech partially satisfies deep needs (connection, efficacy, status, beauty) just enough to prevent the "fed up" moment that triggers change — the doom loop. "The phone isn't the disease; meaninglessness is."
- *Protocol:* a tool where the user names which need a habit is scratching, then picks one real-world replacement ritual tracked via the charging primitive.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens an existing tool category (tool-depth addition, not a new surface). Pairs with the bigger-better-offer intercept below.

**Engineered wonder / craft-project timer → a "make something real" session, charged, no productivity purpose.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* screen-mediated life dulls the nervous system; a serious non-instrumental physical/creative project restores authentic wonder that digital dopamine can't. Tracked by tangible progress, not engagement.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the tool set as a craft/creation session logged through the charging primitive (hold-to-charge = the investment ritual for the session). Extends the tool set rather than bolting on a project manager. Lower priority than the boredom/quiet-brain pair but genuinely ALTER-native.

**Bigger-better-offer intercept → the relief door surfaces the user's OWN named meaningful micro-activities.**
- *Essence / mechanism:* you can't beat distraction with restriction; you need a genuinely more compelling life so shallow distraction looks obviously inferior. "You need a bigger, better offer."
- *Protocol:* at the moment of reaching for distraction, surface the user's own pre-named meaningful-activity list as the one-tap alternative.
- *ALTER hook:* deepens the energy-door / micro-stack routing at exactly its existing intercept point — the relief door offers the user's OWN better offer, not a generic relief tool. This is the highest-value idea in the tools cluster because it lands on an intercept ALTER already owns.

## 2. HIS VOICE — how Cal Newport sells an idea

This section is a direct input to the SCRIPT-ENGINE. Newport is not a canon roster thinker for ALTER, but his *rhetorical move set* is one of the cleanest available for the productivity/attention dialect of ALTER's thesis. Where ALTER's VOICE-BIBLE says "name the false villain, then hand the craftsman alternative," Newport is the reference implementation.

### Register
Calm, contrarian-but-reasonable systems-thinker. He diagnoses modern malaise (burnout, distraction, overload) as a **structural or mathematical mismatch**, never a moral failing, then hands an almost boringly practical fix. Warm but unsentimental — he'd rather give you a system than a pep talk, and he'll cite a Fields medalist's three hours or a novelist's word count before he cites inspiration.

This register is already 90% aligned with ALTER's guardian: "you are not broken, you are misaligned" is Newport's "it's not X, it's Y" wearing ALTER's colors. The difference: the guardian is *intimate* (it watches THIS user's days), Newport addresses a general "we." So when borrowing his moves, add the Chronicle device — the private cite only the guardian could make.

### The framing patterns (the reusable machinery)
1. **Name the false villain, then reveal the real structural culprit.** Villain = willpower / laziness / hours worked / "the tool." Real culprit = overhead / context-switching / pseudo-productivity / bad design. This maps 1:1 to the VOICE-BIBLE "systemic exoneration then fix."
2. **"It's not X, it's Y"** — reframe a character problem as a design problem in one clean beat.

4. **Permission-to-do-less** — explicit, guilt-free license to cut scope, backed by a mechanism, not willpower. (This is ALTER's floor/exit-everywhere principle in Newport's voice.)
5. **The prerequisite stack** — before the exciting layer (vision, deep life) comes the boring foundational layer (discipline, capability). Sequencing is the lesson. (Maps to the journey's discipline-gates-vision ordering.)
6. **Concrete-over-abstract vision** — replace vague goals with sensory, first-person lifestyle descriptions. (This IS ALTER's "a moment, never a category" craft device.)
7. **The exemplar-as-proof move** — a specific real person's real number grounds an abstract claim (June Huh's 3 hours, Grisham's 5am, Watterson's walk-away).

### The rhetorical moves
- **The inversion** — flip the intuitive causal arrow (less produces more; friction is a feature; passion follows mastery). This is ALTER's Inversion Seal, already a VOICE-BIBLE device.
- **The concrete number** — a specific quantity makes the claim falsifiable and memorable (four-hour deep-work cap, 117 emails/day). ALTER's "data beats adjectives."
- **Myth-then-reveal** — state the common assumption, produce the counter-fact. (Great for a stone's PRIME.)
- **The diagnostic reframe** — relabel a felt problem with a precise new name so it becomes actionable (pseudo-productivity, overhead spiral). NOTE: ALTER's rule is "channel the mechanism, never name the concept on screen" — so borrow the *reframe move* but keep the label off the card; the theory-name deals later via a deck card, not in the whisper.
- **Systemic exoneration** — tell the reader they're not broken, THEN offer the fix. This is compassion-before-diagnosis, craft device 3.
