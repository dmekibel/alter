## PART 1: CONVERGENT LAWS (3+ books independently agree; treat as near-law)

1. **Never-punish is clinical-grade, not a brand value.** (MI: "retire the concept of relapse," the prosecutor's-closing anti-pattern, sustain talk predicts non-change · Neff: doughnut study, shame produces worse choices and blocks accountability · Deci: limit-setting is not punishment; pressure yields compliance-or-defiance, both unfree · Fogg: demotivators morph into guilt, "people change best by feeling good" · McCabe: punishment measurably backfires for ADHD brains · Harris: self-criticism = short-term motivation, long-term burnout · Pressfield: the blocker is impersonal, not a verdict.) **Seven+ books.** The moat now has citations; sell it as effectiveness, not kindness.
2. **Evoke, never install (the guardian's deepest law).** (MI: the midwife metaphor, directional reflections, people believe what they hear themselves say · Coaching Habit: the Advice Monster, ask-first fallback · Deci: conditions for self-motivation, self-assessment-first · Make It Stick: generation before instruction · Pennebaker: the user's own narrative is the active ingredient · Yapko: process suggestions let the user supply the content.) Every surface that offers an insight must first ask for the user's.
3. **The last beat is the session.** (Yapko: recency, the last 10-15 seconds become the whole memory · Kahneman peak-end via Coaching Habit: end on the Learning Question · Eason: one constant exit beat + persistence line, every time · Fogg: celebration must land within seconds.) The close of every session, day, and streak-event is the highest-leverage copy in the app.
4. **Attribute every result to the user's participation.** (Yapko: "taking the magic out... deliberateness on the part of clients" · Eason: the Attribution attitude · MI: "We did it ourselves!" · Deci: autonomy, true self-esteem · Fogg: identity as the multiplier.) Session-close and reward copy says what the user did, never what the tool gave.
5. **Honest mechanism disclosure does not kill the effect.** (Kaptchuk open-label placebo, 5-year follow-up (Robson + Yapko) · Eason: "non-deceptive mega-placebo" · Xygalatas: expectation as the honest bridge, hard boundary at broken bones/tumors · Dispenza refused-section: what survives is epigenetics/neuroplasticity/placebo.) The whole magic layer can be fully honest and still work. Cap every claim at a real proportion; never absolutes (absolutes are nocebo bait for non-responders).
6. **Struggle framed as signal, not failure.** (Make It Stick: desirable difficulties, fluency lies · Deci: optimal challenge, never trivially easy · Robson: reappraisal, the 64-point GRE lift · Xygalatas: effortful-unpleasant = meaningful · Pressfield: resistance points at what matters · McCabe: effort is invisible, name it.) Difficulty gets one honest naming line in copy; ease is not the product.
7. **Fixed structure is the mechanism; content can vary.** (Xygalatas: Danish cadence, the consistency IS the anxiety buffer · Eason: constant A-E shell + constant exit · Yapko: one tonal signature, conditioned over sessions · Fogg: precise anchors, fuzzy ones fail.) Bookends and sessions keep an invariant shape forever.
8. **Feeling and thought must co-occur to encode.** (Dispenza: coherence, intention alone or feeling alone does nothing · Fogg: "emotions create habits, not repetition" · Eason rule 5 + Coué auxiliary emotion · Make It Stick: meaning-made-personal is what sticks.) This is the charge primitive's completion condition and the celebration law's mechanism.
9. **Retrospective processing works; forced or anticipatory processing backfires.** (Pennebaker: writing helps for what already happened, not unknown futures; CISD harmed · MI: the forced-plan motivational crash; readiness signals, never timers · Deci: readiness precedes technique · McCabe: permission to stop.) No surface ever forces reflection or planning on a schedule; the PM close is retrospective by design and always skippable.
10. **Language spec is convergent:** second person always (Eason), permissive not imperative (Yapko, Dispenza, Xygalatas), present tense, state the positive not the negation (Yapko, Eason: the lamppost), "for you" personalization (Coaching Habit), never "should/must/as expected" (Deci), silence held after questions (Coaching Habit), warm return-to-focus with zero judgment (Kabat-Zinn via Yapko).

## PART 2: TENSION RESOLUTIONS (where the briefs contradict; these rulings are binding)

**T1. Fogg's celebrate-everything vs Deci's overjustification vs Yapko's never-affirm-without-use.**
Resolution: celebration = the user's own felt emotion in the moment (Shine, authentic pride), which is internal and safe. Evaluative praise ("great job, as expected") and material rewards contingent on intrinsically-motivated tools are what trigger overjustification. Rule: **the app prompts the feeling, never issues the verdict.** Celebration beats are descriptive + felt ("you showed up for that"); charge/coins attach to mechanical habit triggers, never to reflective/creative tools; strengths are affirmed only with a concrete next use (Yapko/Seligman).

**T2. Yapko/Eason suggestion-craft (installs) vs MI evoke-don't-install.**
The realest tension on the shelf: hypnotic suggestion is literally installation; MI forbids it. Resolution: **split by surface + consent.** Guided-audio sessions are opt-in rituals where the user chose the goal; inside them, suggestion craft is legitimate and should be excellent (yes-set, chaining, process suggestions, carry-forward). Conversational surfaces (check-ins, PM close, planning, guardian chat) are MI territory: evoke, reflect, never suggest outcomes. The user picks the destination (MI); the session drives them there (Yapko). **Never mix registers:** no suggestion-chaining in chat copy, no open-question detours mid-induction.

**T3. Xygalatas's causal opacity ("don't explain the ritual") vs Robson/Yapko/Eason mechanism-honesty ("name the mechanism").**
Direct contradiction. Resolution: **explain once, outside; perform opaque, inside.** The mechanism lives in the info layer (the Science page, a "why this works" line near the feature, onboarding once) — this satisfies open-label honesty. Inside the liturgy itself, no mechanism talk: the session stays causally opaque so the ritual stance can do its work. This is exactly the Kaptchuk structure (told plainly up front, then the ritual performed without meta-commentary).

**T4. Xygalatas's costliness (cost proves sincerity) vs Fogg's tininess (under 30 seconds, remove all friction).**
Resolution: **split by function.** Habit-formation surfaces are Fogg-tiny: starter steps, anchors, trivial re-entries after a lapse. Meaning-making surfaces are deliberately costly: the charge hold, graduation ceremonies, the founding purchase, stone completions. Never make the charge a free tap; never make a habit's entry costly. A surface must know which it is.

**T5. Pennebaker's deep disclosure vs ADHD/crisis protection (McCabe, Neff).**
Resolution: gate by acuity and by the 5-item rumination score. No deep-reflection prompt fires same-day after a logged acute event (the CISD law); stop-anytime clause on every deep surface; heavy material gets contain-and-defer (Yapko), never in-app processing; the bedtime brain-dump is a separate light mode, not the PM close.

**T6. Pressfield's push-through vs McCabe's permission-to-stop.**
Pressfield explicitly dismisses ADHD as a "marketing ploy" — that page is refused outright. Resolution: Resistance-naming applies to **avoidance of chosen, meaningful work when capacity exists**; permission-to-stop governs **capacity itself**. The guardian diagnoses which is which before choosing register (MI readiness signals + Fogg's ability-chain diagnostic answer the question: is this a motivation story or an ability story?). **When uncertain, default to compassion** — a wrong "push" costs more than a wrong "rest" (doughnut-study logic).

**T7. Streaks (David's locked keep-streaks decision) vs Deci/Fogg/MI extrinsic-reward warnings.**
Already mostly resolved by the repair mechanic + two-streak hierarchy. The shelf adds two upgrades: (a) **the quiet-compliance detector** (Deci/Grolnick: introjected and integrated users look identical on completion metrics; occasionally probe "did this feel like yours today?"), and (b) streak copy is never evaluative — it counts, it does not judge (Wiesel: "a witness to describe, not a judge to evaluate").

**T8. Make It Stick's desirable difficulty vs Fogg's simplicity-changes-behavior.**
Resolution: **split by layer.** The action layer (doing the habit) is tiny and frictionless. The learning layer (stones, lessons, skills) is effortful by design: generation-first, spaced, interleaved, struggle-gated. A stone that is easy is teaching nothing; a habit that is hard is dying.

## PART 3: THE UNIFIED GUIDED-SESSION GRAMMAR (the B7 voice spec, assembled from five books)

Every guided session (breath, PMR, meditation, Rewire, Visualisation, stretch-fill) runs one invariant shell:

1. **Yes-set open** — 3-4 undeniably true lines (breath, posture, the fact of sitting) before any suggestion. (Yapko)
2. **Induction** — permissive, sensory-specific, body-anchored ("notice the weight of your shoulders"), never "relax now." (Dispenza/Eason)
3. **Control affirmation** — one line: the user is in control, can lighten or end at any time. (Eason B-phase, Yapko safety law)
4. **Deepen** — Bubble Time as the house default settle-in; synchrony cue (paced breath count). (Eason, Xygalatas)
5. **Deliver** — process suggestions ("a particular feeling you want to grow stronger"), cover-all-possibilities, presupposition, chained "as you... you can..." grammar; content supplied by the user, never prescribed autobiographical scenes; eyes-open path always available. (Yapko)
6. **Wandering return** — warm, zero-judgment, "that's the practice, not a mistake." (Kabat-Zinn via Yapko)
7. **Carry-forward** — one specific, contextualized posthypnotic bridge: "the next time you [named trigger], you can [named response]." Never vague. (Yapko)
8. **Constant exit** — the same closing beat every session, user-paced, with the persistence line ("bringing this with you") and **attribution to the user's participation**. The last 15 seconds are deliberately the best part. (Eason, Yapko, Fogg's in-moment Shine)

**Merged banned-word list for guided/tool copy** (add to `_dev/copy-audit.py`): try, can't, won't, don't, should, shouldn't, must, mustn't, no, lose, will (future-tense), sad, difficult, but (in suggestions) · sleep-metaphors, the charged negative noun repeated back (say sensation/pressure, not pain) · "the universe," "the field," "manifest" · "I am [positive trait]" affirmation-register lines (Harris: they backfire; use noticing/defusion framing) · "as expected," "living up to," any comparative ranking · "relapse," "broken," "failed" · fixed day-counts ("21 days") · absolute outcome claims ("this works," "you'll feel amazing").

**Register split (from T2):** suggestion grammar only inside sessions; MI grammar (open questions, directional reflections, rulers, bouquets, AOA ask-offer-ask) everywhere conversational. One tonal signature for all guided audio, never varied.

## PART 4: REFUSED TECHNIQUES → HONEST SUBSTITUTES (mind+psy shelf)

| Refused | Source + why | Honest substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Quantum/field/entanglement framing, retroactive intention | Dispenza; pseudophysics | Nervous system, gene expression, conditioned physiology, named studies |
| Hypnotic/guided "memory recovery" | Yapko + Eason: produces false memories | Reflection works only with what the user consciously knows |
| Decisional-balance (pros/cons) UI as motivation | MI: decreases commitment in ambivalence | Menu-of-choices picker (2-4 concrete options) |
| Forced plans / "are you ready?" on a timer | MI: documented motivational crash | Readiness-signal gating; importance/confidence rulers |
| Prosecutor's-closing summaries (all-fails list) | MI: demoralizes, increases resistance | Bouquet summary of the user's own change-talk |
| Demotivators: guilt pledges, shame screens, public stakes | Fogg + Neff + McCabe | Starter-step re-entry + normalize the lapse |
| Thought suppression ("don't think about it") | Wegner via Eason/Pennebaker: boomerangs | Defusion ("I'm having the thought that..."), externalization, paradoxical invitation |
| Affirmation register ("I am confident") | Harris: backfires against self-image; Yapko: rejected by dissonance | Noticing language + strength-with-a-concrete-use |
| CISD-style same-day forced debrief | Pennebaker: harmful | Optional, gated, stop-anytime retrospective reflection |
| Future-anxiety journaling sold on EW evidence | Pennebaker: no support pre-outcome | Best-possible-future-self prompt (the one validated future-facing form) |
| "Learning styles" personalization axis | Make It Stick: debunked (Pashler) | Match mode to content, not to claimed preference |
| Pressfield's ADHD-dismissal + shame-as-fuel + love-your-misery | Contradicts the core audience + never-punish | Resistance-naming as impersonal compass, capacity-checked (T6) |
| Stage-hypnosis compliance tactics (flattery-leverage, social pressure) | Yapko: "unethical" outright | Permission, consent, control lines |
| Curse/"manifestation" claims about external outcomes | Xygalatas boundary + Dispenza | Expectation affects stress physiology and behavior, full stop |
