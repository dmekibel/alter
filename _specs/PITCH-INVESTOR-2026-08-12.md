# ALTER investor / partner pitch (2026-08-12)

Built from COMPETITOR-TEARDOWN-8APPS + FINCH-BEND, BUSINESS-MASTERPLAN, FOUNDATION-PITCH, SOUL-OF-ALTER, plus live market research (sources below). Passed Gate 1 (copy-audit) and an adversarial judge pass against COPY-ANCHORS. The spoken pitch is ~2 minutes; the Q&A ammo is for when they push.

## The spoken pitch

**The market shift**

Calm's revenue reportedly dropped about a quarter last year. Headspace lost around 300,000 paid subscribers. Meanwhile Finch, a bootstrapped self-care app about a pixel bird, grew to tens of millions in revenue and about eight million monthly actives with zero venture money. People are tired of content libraries that lecture them. They pay for companions and games.

**What I'm building**

ALTER is a guardian angel life-sim. A pixel-art guardian lives on your phone and runs the day with you. It plans your morning, tracks your habits, walks you through breathing and meditation, and closes your evening. Planners organize your day. ALTER trains the person living it.

**The gaming thesis**

The steadiest earner in mobile is the cozy daily-ritual game. June's Journey has taken over a billion dollars lifetime, mostly from women over 55 playing the same gentle loop every day. Back in 2016, PewDiePie's Tuber Simulator pulled ten million downloads in a single day on pixel art and progress bars. That loop is proven: a cozy world, a daily check-in, visible progression. ALTER points it at your real life. You level up because you actually meditated, actually planned your day and lived it.

**The competitor gap**

Finch proved the companion model scales, and Finch is comfort with no spine: a checklist with a pet on top, no planner, no growth arc, and its endgame is selling outfits for the bird. Habitica gamified the to-do list a decade ago and stopped there. The serious planners, Sunsama, Tiimo, have no companion and no game. Companion plus a real planner is an open lane. ALTER sits in the middle of it, for the people who read Atomic Habits and find Finch childish but Notion cold.

**The moat**

Most of this category retains users with anxiety: streaks, guilt pings, fake urgency. ALTER retains the way a gym does, with depth. The engine is a tool system built from a curated knowledge base, 34 thinkers distilled into breathing, meditation, focus and rewiring techniques that compose into stacks and whole day templates. It trains one habit at a time until it runs on autopilot, then moves to the next, and the guardian matures with you: it starts by teaching, and years in it's citing your own record back to you. People keep a practice like that for life. And there is no streak shame anywhere in it, no dark patterns, no dependency manipulation. The FTC has been fining subscription dark patterns nine figures and investigating AI companions for engineering dependency, so the honest architecture is also the safe one.

**Where it stands, and why me**

The app is real and I live in it. Over 1,250 shipped versions, built solo, running on my phone every day as my own planner and tracker. The knowledge system behind it took years to assemble before the first line of code. It ships as a web app with Stripe checkout, so no App Store commission at launch. Revenue opens with a founding lifetime deal in three tranches at 49, 69, and 99 dollars, then settles into a free core that stays free plus a premium depth tier. The planned paid magnet is an AI goal architect that costs cents per user per month to run, so we're modeling margins in the 70 to 90 percent range, on near-zero burn. Underneath all of it, mental health apps are a nine to ten billion dollar market growing double digits, inside a fifty-plus billion dollar self-improvement market.

## Q&A ammo (numbers, sources, honesty flags)

- Self-improvement market: $53B (2025), Precedence Research, Feb 2026. Other firms go up to $70B. Say "fifty billion plus."
- Calm decline: ~$210M 2025 revenue, down ~24% YoY. FLAG: single source (Latka aggregator). Always say "reportedly."
- Headspace: ~300K paid subs lost in 2025 (teardown doc). The $3B valuation floating around is the stale 2021 Ginger-merger figure. Skip Headspace valuations entirely.
- Finch: $30-40M ARR claimed, bootstrapped, ~8.3M actives, 75% women 25-35, 4.9 stars across 550-723K ratings. FLAG: Sensor Tower's Q2 2025 app-store-only figure (~$380K/mo) conflicts with the ARR claim (likely different tracking scope). Say "tens of millions" and skip the precise number.
- June's Journey: $1B+ cumulative lifetime revenue (Playtika IR, Nov 2023); ~90% women, majority 55+. FLAG: that is a lifetime total. Quote it as lifetime only.
- Tuber Simulator: 10M downloads in ~24h at its 2016 launch. FLAG: legacy title now. Use it as proof of pixel-art progression demand, and keep the year in the sentence.
- Category shallowness: only ~2% of wellness apps carry any peer-reviewed evidence (PMC 2020, "Science or Snake Oil"). That's the KB-credibility card. A Frontiers in Psychiatry review (Apr 2025) found zero apps that build real user independence. Use this ONLY as evidence the category farms dependency. NEVER pitch ALTER as designed to be quit or needed less (killed concept: daily lifetime use is the intended healthy pattern; skills go autopilot, the practice and tools are for life).
- If they frame lifetime daily use as dependency: dependency is manipulation-driven retention (HBS: 43% of AI-companion farewells manipulate, re-engagement up to 14x). ALTER's retention is ritual and depth, the gym frame: nobody calls a gym membership a dependency. The app has no feed, no engagement KPIs, no guilt pings.
- Dark patterns: FTC/ICPEN 2024 sweep found ~76% of subscription apps use at least one; Amazon fined $2.5B (2025), Adobe $150M (2026).
- AI-companion crisis: Character.AI wrongful-death suits (2024-25); MIT/OpenAI RCT (n=981) linking heavy chatbot use to loneliness and dependence; HBS study: 43% of AI-companion farewell messages use emotional manipulation, boosting re-engagement up to 14x; FTC 6(b) inquiry into 7 companies.
- Retention base rates (when they ask "how do you beat the churn"): wellness median D1 ~25%, D7 ~10%, D30 ~5%. ALTER's designed answer is a real first-session win (the day-one micro-loop) plus a companion with memory, which is the mechanic Finch proved retains.
- Projections (when they ask): planned against base rates. The modal indie wellness outcome in 90 days is $0; the plan targets first $1,000 cumulative by day 60-90 via the founding deal and $1K MRR by month 6-9. Anything faster is upside. Saying this out loud reads as discipline, and it is true.
- Why gamification usually fails (if they cite it): a 36-RCT meta-analysis found modest effects, and the converting mechanics (streaks as loss-aversion) backfire hardest on the anxious and burned-out, who are the actual audience. Duolingo's own Streak Freeze cut churn 21%, an admission the raw streak was hurting. ALTER bans the shame mechanics and keeps the world-state reward, which is the one mechanic that works honestly.
