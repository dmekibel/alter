## 0. THE DAD LAW (why this doc exists)
When David was asked "what is the app," the indirect answer failed and the plain one landed: **a productivity app, a habit tracker, and a mental health app.** That is now a law of the pitch: **lead with the category, earn the poetry.** Strangers (and dads, and App Store browsers) place you by category first. The guardian, the game, the grimoire: all of it comes second, as the *how*, never the *what*.

This matches the research: the meta-finding of the whole strategy corpus is that ALTER "does not have a strategy problem, it has a shipping/distribution problem wearing a strategy costume" (MASTER-SYNTHESIS). The pitch was never missing. It was scattered across 16 docs. This doc is the condensation.

## 2. THE VISION STATEMENT (v2, corrected per David 2026-07-11)

**For people who want to get their life in order and are put off by apps that baby them, ALTER is the daily planner, habit tracker, and mental fitness trainer in one. It makes planning your day, your week, and your month fun, quick, and a lot less intimidating. It trains real habits one at a time until they run on autopilot, then moves to the next with you: the ladder is a year deep, drawn from a curated canon of real practices (morning design, shutdown rituals, breathwork, focus systems). It never punishes a missed day, and it respects your intelligence: every technique explains its mechanism, and names its source when you ask.**

*(Thinker names stay OUT of the forefront per David 2026-07-11: strangers do not know Johnson or Newport, and the app must stand on its own. The sources live one tap deep inside the app as earned credibility, not as the pitch.)*

*(Category term "guided mastery course" is a PLACEHOLDER: David's dad flagged "self-help" as too broad; market research on the category term is running. Same for "mental-health toolkit" in §1.)*

**DAVID'S CORRECTION (load-bearing, 2026-07-11): never sell "you will use it less."** The point of the app is NOT reduced use. The graduation research (§4) stays as internal design philosophy (per-habit coaching matures), but the PITCH frames it as **habits on autopilot plus an endless next frontier**: habits graduate, the user never does, the app is the planner and companion forever. The guardian never runs out of job because the mastery ladder (Johnson's yearlong course, Newport's stacked systems) is always deeper than the user's current rung.

**THE REGISTER: the Apple of the category.** Finch wins its lane by being soft to the point of childish (the plush-toy register, comfort-seeking buyer). ALTER does not compete for that buyer. ALTER's register is classy, thought-out, clever, clean, and it respects the user: mechanism-first copy, named sources, no baby-talk, no exclamation marks, no mascot voice. Design-language translation: the game-piece component law, the moody matte field, scarce reward shine (already locked in the constitution) ARE this register; the copy must match them.

Unlike every funded competitor, ALTER can make its promise honestly, because their business models require the opposite (engagement farmed, lapses shamed, dependence engineered). This is not a slogan; it is the one seam the research found structurally uncontested (see §4).

The three commitments underneath it, each a standing law in the constitution:
1. **One coherent daily loop, not a bundle of tabs.** Plan → live → close, one spine (the three-pane app, the cockpit, the timeline). Competitors are drawers of 5 unrelated tabs; the loop is the product.
2. **Real techniques, honestly sourced.** The tools are faithful protocols from a curated canon (Johnson, Stutz, Maltz, breathwork, PMR, EFT), taught at the moment of need, with the mechanism explained. ~2% of wellness apps have any evidence behind them; the credibility floor of the category is on the ground.
3. **Reward, never shame.** No streaks that break, no red, no guilt pings. Drift is data. The return after a gap is the most celebrated moment in the app.

## 3. THE CORE AND WHAT SERVES IT (the anti-scatter map)

**THE CORE USE CASE: the compounding daily loop.** Plan your day. Live it against a timeline that visibly charges as you do what you planned. Close it at night, and let what actually happened make tomorrow's plan better. That loop is the app. A user who does only this, forever, is a fully successful user.

**DAVID'S CORE STATEMENT (2026-07-11, verbatim, the spine of all pitch copy per the verbatim-anchor law):** "Doing Cal Newport style daily planner and habit tracker and making long term goals sounds good on paper, but in practice it's hard to do and hard to organize yourself. And there's no one guided system for how to do it. So this app brings the most cutting edge productivity techniques to your fingertips and makes it much easier to be an organized person. It's like Google Calendar on steroids... it helps you get organized and live your best life. Like having your own personal Jim Rohn in your pocket."

The revolution, extracted: **the techniques were never the product; they are known and free. The missing thing in the world is the guided system that runs them with you, daily.** ALTER is that system. Note the built-in emotional safety: the problem statement blames the missing system, never the user (compassion before diagnosis, structurally). The two analogies are SPOKEN-register assets ("Google Calendar on steroids" for instant category placement; "a mentor in your pocket" as the de-named Rohn shape), never store metadata.

**THE DAD THESIS (2026-07-11, now load-bearing):** get the habits right and everything else falls into place: mental health, career, goals. Productivity comes down entirely to *plan the day, do it, plan a better one tomorrow.* So the planner-tracker loop is the foundation the app pushes hardest; meditation and breathing are core but SECONDARY, in service of the loop (they repair the state so the day can continue). EVIDENCE: this is the keystone-habit thesis from the habit-formation literature (Duhigg); it is the teardown's system-level finding ("the daily loop IS the product": loops retain, bundles churn); and it is Cal Newport's entire corpus (the mine already ranked the shutdown-ritual PM bookend as build #1). Three independent sources and the dad converge.

**DIRECTION CONFIRMED — DE-GAME THE APP (David, 2026-07-11):** "we want people to be productive, not be more on their phone playing games." His articulation of what remains, near-verbatim: **the app is a game in the sense that doing what you planned gets you the most points, and you spend that on rewards, cosmetics, and unlocks.** So gamification lives in exactly two layers: (1) THE CHARGE (the Guitar Hero feel: doing what you planned lights the timeline; the core, SELL it; spoken ledger: "Guitar Hero for your day," never store metadata) and (2) COSMETIC + UNLOCK ECONOMY (the Tuber Simulator model: charge discharges into looks, plants, gifts, and earned unlocks; never gates the core, never a second loop). CUT: any world-you-enter with its own game loop (AUDIT already called it "a points counter, not a game you want to open"). The guardian is untouched: it was never the game. ONE ECONOMY LAW: the charge IS the coin; never introduce a second currency. Build implication: subtraction pass on the game surfaces, scoped in its own session.

**STREAK DECISION (David, 2026-07-11, reverses the earlier "no streaks" stance):** Keep streaks, remove the punishment. The evidence pointed at the shatter-and-shame, never at the streak itself (the forward-pull is real, valuable loss aversion). TWO streaks only, with hierarchy (never a 5-counter stats page): (1) **"Planned Ahead" = the HERO** streak the guardian talks about: did you plan tomorrow before it arrived. This IS the PM close. It is the keystone habit (Newport shutdown / dad's thesis) AND our single strongest science (implementation intentions, Gollwitzer d=0.65). (2) **"Followed Through" = the quiet companion**: did you do what you planned; already tied to the timeline charge. REPAIR MECHANIC (David's pick, option B): a miss never zeroes the streak; a short make-up window reconnects the chain (repairing IS the rep, the catch-is-the-unit thesis); planned rest days keep it lit (alignment, not raw completion); reuses the existing Return/Relight mechanic, no new system. The plan/follow-through pair = last-night's-promise vs today-keeping-it (the time-symmetry / letter-across-time idea). COPY CHANGE: the App Store line "No streaks to lose" becomes provisional "A streak you can't lose by accident" (stronger hook; gate before shipping). Not yet built.

**The under-built half, named honestly:** the app is strong at *plan → live* (the timeline is the loved leg) and thin at *close → better plan* (the PM bookend that reads today's record and seeds tomorrow). Dad's thesis makes that the highest-priority gap in the whole product: the compounding is the value, and the compounding happens at the close.

Everything else in the app exists to keep that loop alive, and must justify itself by naming which failure of the loop it prevents:

| Feature | The loop-failure it prevents | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **The burning timeline** | "I plan but never see it count." The now-line converts plan into lived record; progress is visible, not abstract. | The core surface. Built, loved. |
| **Tools (breath, PMR, mantra, tapping, meditation)** | "I abandoned the plan because my state collapsed." Tools repair the state so the day can continue. Sixty seconds, body-first. | Built, deepened 07-08. |
| **The guardian** | "I opened the app and did not know what to do." It reads the moment and hands you ONE next step. It carries the load of choosing. | Partial (routing thin). |
| **The journey (stones)** | "I never learned to use any of it." The journey is the tutorial: it teaches one tool at a time, by doing, in order. | Stone 1 in build. |
| **The game layer (charge, world, rewards)** | "Doing the plan feels like nothing." Charging makes the rep felt; the world reflects a life actually lived. | Partial. |
| **Bookends (AM/PM)** | "Days start unaimed and end unexamined." The two hinges of the loop itself. | Thin. |

**The refusal list (features ALTER will NOT have, on evidence):** breakable streaks (backfire on the anxious/ADHD audience: Lancet meta-analysis, Duolingo's own Streak Freeze data), completion-percent guilt meters, social feeds and leaderboards, a content-library tab (knowledge decoupled from the moment of need is dead weight), paywalls before value, fake proof, notification nagging. Every one converts well and rots the exact user this app serves.

**The scatter test (run on every new idea):** which loop-failure does it prevent? If the answer takes more than one sentence, it does not go in. This is Principle 1 (CONDENSE) and the no-random-features law, now with the dad's framing: core first, satellites serve the core.

## 4. POSITIONING (where ALTER stands in the market)

**The market:** mental-wellness apps ~$9-10B (2025) → ~$17-23B by 2030. The subscription-meditation giants are shrinking (Calm revenue -24% YoY; Headspace lost ~300K paid subs in 2025). The growth story is Finch: a gentle self-care companion, bootstrapped to ~$30-40M ARR with zero VC and a 4.9-star rating, proving **honest wins in this category.**

**The white space (research-precise, two journal-grade findings):**
- Only ~2% of wellness apps carry any peer-reviewed evidence ("Science or Snake Oil," PMC).
- A direct literature review found **zero apps designed to reduce their own use or foster user independence** (Frontiers in Psychiatry, Apr 2025). The leaders are academically named for addiction mechanics.

So the uncontested position from the teardown, INTERNAL framing: a guardian that resonates like Finch and goes as deep as a companion, designed for internalization instead of dependence. Finch is gentle but shallow (and deliberately childish). Replika is deep but engineered to hold you. Tiimo is clinical but cold. Calm is content with no relationship. **Nobody is deep, respectful of the user, and designed to make habits genuinely yours, because their revenue depends on engineered dependence.** ALTER's positioning costs the competitors their business model to copy. That is what makes it defensible.

**PITCH TRANSLATION of that white space (per David's correction):** never "you will use it less." Instead: *it trains habits until they run on their own, then moves to the next; the ladder is a year deep.* The anti-dependence design is real and stays; the sold promise is autopilot habits plus endless depth, with the app as your planner and companion throughout.

**AUDIENCE (sharpened 2026-07-11):** NOT Finch's comfort-seeking plush-toy buyer. ALTER sells to people who want their life run with intention and are put off by baby-talk: the optimizer, the professional, the person who reads James Clear and watches Newport. Premium register is the filter that finds them.

**Adjacent gaps ALTER also occupies:** companion + real daily planner in one (the two categories live on opposite sides today); warmth + executive-function rigor (Tiimo's own users name this gap); no-dark-patterns as the brand (now FTC-enforced: 76% of subscription apps use them; Amazon paid $2.5B).

**Who it is for (the four acceptance personas):** the overwhelmed doer who needs relief before structure; the plain-language parent (RU-first); the skeptical optimizer who wants mechanisms, not woo; the structured doer who wants the day aimed and nothing else. A feature ships only when it serves all four.

## 6. THE RESEARCH LAW (dad's second rule, made operational)

**The rule: no product decision without one line of evidence.** Four admissible kinds, in order of strength:
1. **A real user on a real phone** (the strongest; also the scarcest right now).
2. **David's own usage data** (the app already logs it; n=1 is still evidence).
3. **A competitor pattern observed directly** (the teardown corpus: 10 recordings, 8 apps).
4. **Published research** (the market-research threads; journal-grade preferred, vendor stats flagged).

**Mechanics:** every decision recorded in a handoff or spec carries an `EVIDENCE:` line naming its source. A decision that cannot name one is a hypothesis, and hypotheses get tested cheapest-first, not shipped as convictions.

**What this doc's own claims rest on:** every number and positioning claim in §4 traces to COMPETITOR-TEARDOWN-8APPS (which flags vendor-sourced stats explicitly). The core-loop primacy traces to the teardown's system-level synthesis (the loops retain, the bundles churn). The refusal list traces to the Lancet gamification meta-analysis and the streak-backfire literature.

**The single highest-leverage research move (already named in MASTER-SYNTHESIS, now dad-endorsed):** one real second user on one real phone. The four personas exist as test users in the family. The PMF interview spec exists (SPEC-PMF-INTERVIEW-2026-07-02.md). Running one sibling through the first day and watching, saying nothing, is worth more than any further document, including this one.

## 6.5 THE MONETIZATION MODEL (sister's June's-Journey-inspired proposal, 2026-07-11, filtered through the refusal list)

The proposal: app is free; daily quests/challenges unlock paid features temporarily ("then they feel like they earned it"); high VIP tier anchoring a cheaper tier; daily surprise boxes; coins; outfits; plants; membership discounts; upgraded meditation as paid depth. Sorted against the locked laws (free buys transformation; paid buys expression/depth, never access; no dark patterns; reward the lived day, never the visit):

**HONEST AS-IS:**
- **Outfits, plants, cosmetic economy** = exactly the confirmed de-game direction (charge discharges into looks and growth; Finch proved cosmetic-only premium reaches $30-40M ARR with zero dark patterns).
- **Upgraded meditation / paid depth** = the paid-buys-depth law, verbatim.
- **Membership discounts earned by practice** = transparent, flattering, honest.
- **Coins** = the charge itself. ONE ECONOMY: never a second currency.

**HONEST WITH A TWIST (the twist is what keeps it off the FTC's list):**
- **Earn-to-unlock paid content through practice** = the strongest idea in the list. It is teardown move N (reward engagement with personalization/access, not a paywall nag) fused with the June's-Journey earned-pride loop. The twist: **the quest IS your own plan.** The unlock triggers when a planned block charges (a lived hour), never from screen-time tasks, check-in taps, or synthetic challenges. ALTER never invents busywork to farm opens: your day is the quest.
- **"Temporarily"** = the dark edge of the idea. Loss-framed expiry (countdown timers, "expiring soon!") is manufactured urgency: the exact mechanic the FTC sweep names and the anxious audience churns from. The honest frame: **a gift with a horizon.** "You earned three days of the full mantra library" reads as a taste and an invitation; no countdowns, no expiry pings, it just quietly returns to locked with warmth. Earned-trial, not slipping-away possession.
- **Daily surprise box / gift** = the honest-variable-reward law: FIXED whether (the gift always comes when the day closes), VARIABLE content (which thinker's line, which cosmetic, which taste-unlock). Attach it to the PM close (the lived day), never to the app open (the visit). Evolve's end-of-session envelope, done truthfully.
- **High VIP anchor tier** (David's metaphor, 2026-07-11: "offering the expensive steak so the less expensive yet still expensive pasta feels less in comparison, so the user feels like they got a deal") = legitimate and universal (menus, Apple's own storage tiers, the annual-vs-monthly norm). The honest/dark line: **the steak must be a real steak.** A top tier with genuine added value that some users actually buy (the natural candidate: the bespoke guardian tier: real voice via ElevenLabs, BYO-AI brain, full depth + expression: genuinely costly to serve, genuinely premium) = honest anchoring. A fake decoy priced never to sell, pre-selected checkout, fake strikethrough prices = the FTC sweep's named pattern. Rule: ALTER may set the menu; it never fakes the menu.

**THE ONE CONTRADICTION TO HOLD (David said both in one breath):** "we want people productive, not more on their phone" AND "incentivize people to use the app daily." Resolution, and it is the whole model in one sentence: **incentivize living the plan daily, never visiting the app daily.** The quest completes off-screen, when the planned hour is lived; the app is where you collect, not where you grind. Every mechanic above passes or fails by that test.
