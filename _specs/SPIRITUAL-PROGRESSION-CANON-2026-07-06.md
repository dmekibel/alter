## The core architecture: a PSYCHOGRAPH, not a "spiritual level"

Nobody has one spiritual level. David's own framing — *"you might love meditation but be bad at basic attention"* — is exactly **Ken Wilber's lines of development**: each practice is a **semi-independent skill with its own ladder**, and you can be advanced in one and a beginner in another. So ALTER's spiritual engine is a **psychograph**: a per-line profile the app builds by reading your vibe, with a diagnostic ladder per line that hands you the next rung.

**Use Wilber (and Kegan) for the *architecture only*** — they give the multi-line concept but **no operational assessment instrument** (Wilber is a taxonomy; Kegan's Subject-Object Interview is a trained-rater hours-long qualitative process, not automatable). The *ladders* come from the practice-specific maps below.

This is the spiritual instantiation of ALTER's already-canon "per-skill Duolingo ladders." It deepens the journey; it doesn't bolt on.

## The assessment engine — how it "catches your vibe" (no test)

The app builds the psychograph per line through three channels, blended — never a quiz:
1. **Light placement (one-time, per line, optional):** 2–3 self-report questions phrased as recognition, not exam ("When you try to hold your attention on your breath, what usually happens?" → maps to a TMI stage band). Skippable; the app will re-place you from behavior anyway.
2. **Behavioral vibe-reading (the real signal):** which practices you gravitate to, how long you actually sustain a session, completion vs. bail, self-rated "how did that land," time-of-day patterns, the energy-door read. This is the honest version of "catches on to your vibes" — it watches what you *do*, not what you *claim*.
3. **Adaptive next-rung:** hand the next rung, watch if it lands (did you sustain? did it feel forced?). If it's too hard, drop back a rung; if trivially easy, skip up. Classic adaptive-difficulty, per line. TMI's stage self-report questions are the gating logic; Shinzen's axes are the meters.

The guardian *speaks* the psychograph back ("your attention's steadier than it was — you're holding past the first fidget now"), which is the recognition-reward from the June's Journey study, pointed at real skill.

## The honest / science framing (non-negotiable — it's ALTER's differentiator)

**Science-content, esoteric-form.** The meditation and hypnosis lines are empirically grounded; the magic lines are framed as **psychological technique**, not supernatural claim:
- **Magic = psychology is the mechanism, not a metaphor for one.** Academic framings to use as the app's own language: symbolic-action/**placebo** (Lévi-Strauss, Glucklich), **performativity** (J.L. Austin — ritually stating intent makes it psychologically "true" for the actor), and **anxiety-relief via "goal demotion"** (Keinan; Boyer & Liénard — ritual works by shifting attention from an uncontrollable outcome onto controllable precise gestures, restoring felt agency). Placebo-neuroplasticity (PMC 2024) is a legit hook for "ritual belief produces measurable brain change" without overclaiming.
- **Label honestly.** Sigil mechanisms (priming/Zeigarnik/attentional-bias) are *plausible borrowed analogies, not tested for sigils* → present as "psychological framing," never "proven mechanism." **Hypnotic susceptibility is the one validated instrument** in the esoteric set — treat it with more empirical confidence than the rest. This labeling *is* the trust moat (the "only 2% of wellness apps have evidence" differentiator).

### Safety gate (responsible-design requirement)
The insight/awakening line (MCTB ñāṇas, stages 5–10 "Dark Night") carries **real adverse-event risk** (Britton / Cheetah House meditation-harm research). The advanced insight track must be **opt-in, safety-gated**, with "pause and stabilize" checkpoints, an off-ramp, and never pushed on a novice or a user in a low/vulnerable state (the energy-door already reads this). Concentration (TMI) and the tools are safe defaults; deep insight is the one place ALTER must *slow* a user down, not gamify them forward.

## MEDITATION LANE SYSTEM — confirmed build spec (2026-07-14, David-decided)

**Origin:** David steered the "content-depth pass" here after studying how Sam Harris structures LONG sits (real transcripts, playlist PLRSXPjY6egnmuqYz-9rAIe7VM4sUoDw-P). Two corrections from David that define it:
1. **Depth is a CONSCIOUS lane choice, never a length side-effect.** The user picks the lane (= their ceiling); length only deepens WITHIN that lane. A long Anchor sit gets richer *concentration*, never dumped into self-inquiry.
2. **Ground the progression in the canon books, not invented tiers** (TMI/Shinzen/Ingram + Harris/Kelly nondual content).

### Measured Harris findings (from 9/26/38-min transcripts)
- Working pace stays ~constant (**~11.5s mean** between cues) regardless of length. He does NOT balloon silence.
- Longer = MORE distinct content (42 cues @26min vs 68 @38min) covering DEEPER objects, not stretched repeats.
- The longest silences (30–50s) fire only after the DEEPEST prompts (self-inquiry), to let you investigate.
- Arc: body → breath → open awareness → awareness-as-space → the mirror (a bad memory can't stain you) → the head/looking illusion → "where is the self / the thinker" → felt demos (picture X then Y, they arise+pass, which were you?). He OSCILLATES back to the breath as home base between deep moves. Every deep move is an EXPERIMENT you feel, never an assertion.
- **Engine correction this implies:** our current engine balloons silence to fill long sits (wrong). Fix: constant ~11s pace; length walks further down the lane's ladder (more content); long silences ONLY on deep prompts.

### The 5 lanes (UI: named by the skill they train — David picked this)
| Lane (label) | Canon ladder | Length deepens by |
|---|---|---|
| **Concentration** | Culadasa TMI 10 stages | find breath → whole cycle → sensations INSIDE the breath → effortless attention, breath in foreground / everything else calm background |
| **Mindfulness** | Shinzen see/hear/feel noting | widen the field, finer noting of sounds/sensations/thoughts as objects |
| **Open awareness** | equanimity / choiceless | longer silences, less doing, rest as the space things arise in |
| **Insight** (OPT-IN, GATED) | Ingram + Harris/Kelly/Adyashanti | the mirror → "where is the thinker" → felt demos, with a gentle off-ramp |
| **Heart** | metta / loving-kindness | widening circle of warmth |

### Rules (binding)
- User picks lane = conscious depth choice. Length deepens WITHIN lane along its ladder, never crosses to a deeper lane's ceiling automatically.
- **Insight is opt-in + safety-gated** (Britton/Cheetah House Dark Night risk, per this canon): one-time opt-in gate, gentle off-ramp, NEVER suggested to a beginner or when the energy-door read is low.
- Constant ~11s working pace; long silences reserved for the deepest prompts.
- Depth only gets *suggested* in the "average" stack-suggestion surface (full stack / meditation stack) — reads vibe + energy-door, proposes a lane, user always chooses.
- All new lines pass BOTH gates + the amalgam law + copyright pivot (technique is free; a living teacher's phrasing is not). Guided voice = tradition's common tongue, nuance ceiling ≈ Harris/Adyashanti, never Claude-clever.
- **Phase 2 (the clever payoff):** guardian speaks the psychograph back (Shinzen meters: "your attention's steadier, you're holding past the first fidget").

### GATED CONTENT BANKED (both gates passed; ready to wire)
**Concentration / breath pool (tiered find→sensation):**
1. There's no need to control it or deepen it. The body knows how to breathe. Just let it come and go.
2. Feel where the breath is clearest right now, and let your attention rest there.
3. Notice whether it's long or short, deep or shallow. However it is right now is fine.
4. Follow one full breath, from the start of the in-breath, through the pause, to the end of the out-breath.
5. Notice the small pause at the top of the in-breath, and the one after you breathe out.
6. You don't have to change anything. Just stay with each breath as it comes.
7. Notice the breath as sensation now. The cooler air coming in, a little warmer going out.
8. See if you can catch the start of the next in-breath, before it fully arrives.
9. When you notice your mind has wandered, that's the practice. Gently come back to the breath.

**Insight / Looking pool (self-inquiry ceiling, in ALTER's own words):**
1. Now we'll try something different. Instead of watching your experience, look for the one who is watching it.
2. Notice the next thought the moment it appears. It arrives on its own, and it passes on its own.
3. You didn't choose it or build it. It simply showed up, and now it's gone.
4. Bring to mind something that bothered you recently. Let the feeling come, and be the space it appears in.
5. Hold it the way a mirror holds a reflection. Fully, but without being changed by it.
6. Now let a good memory come instead. Notice the mind brighten, and notice you are the one who is aware, while the mood itself comes and goes.
7. Picture a place you know. Now picture someone's face. Each one appears, then it's gone. Which one was you?
8. You feel like the thinker behind your thoughts. Look for that thinker now. Is there anything there but the next thought?
9. Look for the self, the one you call I. Is there a center to all this, or is there just the experience itself?
10. You won't find a thing there, and that's fine. Rest as the awareness all of it is appearing in.
