## The tension (stated plainly)
- Not everyone wants a spiritual journey, but the app is a guardian built to guide one.
- Not everyone knows their goals, but the app wants to help them reach their goals.
- Not everyone wants the Brian Johnson course, but the app wants to guide everyone through it.
- And right now the built app is too complicated: tools are hard to reach, controls are tiny, people do not know how to use them.

These look like two different problems (a philosophy problem and a UI problem). They are the same problem, and they have one fix.

## The resolution
**The app is opinionated about the tools, not the destination.** It is certain these tools work. It is completely humble about what you want to use them for. It never has a destination it drags you toward. Your goal is the north star, and the app's wisdom serves that, not the other way around.

## The guardian is the perfect parent (David's frame, 2026-07-06)
A perfect parent does three things and knows which the moment calls for:
1. **Encourages** (warmth, belief in you, celebrates the real thing).
2. **Gives space** (does not smother, lets you choose, lets you rest, never forces).
3. **Gives tools and advice** (when you reach for help, hands you the right tool and the real wisdom, and teaches you to use it).

The skill is reading which one this moment needs. Sometimes a push. Sometimes a hug. Sometimes to be left alone. Sometimes a specific tool for a specific problem. A perfect parent never neglects and never controls. Those are the two failure modes, and they are exactly the two failures of the market:
- **Neglect** = a shallow tracker that gives you a checkbox and no relationship.
- **Control** = the engineered-dependence apps (Replika and friends) that smother and never let you go.

ALTER is neither. It stays (never hands you over) and it frees (never coerces). The whole app is the perfect parent rendered in software, and its intelligence is knowing, for this person right now, whether to encourage, give space, or give a tool.

## The five moves that make it real (each already in our canon)
1. **Relief is a complete, worthy endpoint, not a doorway.** The person who just wants to stop feeling awful gets exactly that, fully, forever, with no guilt-trip toward "growth." A calm nervous system is a finished outcome. The floor is always there and is never treated as failure. (The "always a way out" leg of the soul doc.)
2. **Depth is pulled, never pushed.** The Johnson course, the meditation ladder, the awakening work, the magic, the full goals architecture are all present and none are forced. They surface only as the person shows appetite (does more, lingers, reaches). "Guide everyone through it" becomes "make it available to everyone, reveal it to those who reach for it."
3. **Lead with the universal, name the spiritual only for those who want it.** A breath is just a breath until someone wants it to be more. The skeptic gets a sharp, secular, effective tool. The seeker gets a grimoire. Same tool, different frame, different depth. Nobody gets woo they did not ask for. (Science-content, esoteric-form, applied product-wide.)
4. **You do not need to know your goals to start.** The app helps you find your one small want through use. One want is enough. It never demands a life plan as the price of entry.
5. **The adaptation is the product.** One-size-fits-all through great adaptation. The genius is not a single perfect path, it is that the app reads you and becomes the right app for you. Same soul, feels custom-built for each person.

The honest reframe of the app's ambition: it does not want to make everyone a monk or a Johnson graduate. It wants to get each person meaningfully closer to what they actually want, with the best tools that exist, at the depth they choose. Its only non-negotiable opinion is "these work, and I will never force them on you."

## Prescriptive by default, subtractive by choice, adaptive on top (David, 2026-07-07)
Important balance, so "depth is pulled not pushed" is never misread as a passive app that waits for you to ask. **Most people do not know what to do.** Decision paralysis and a blank slate are the real enemy. So the app leads:
1. **Prescriptive by default (additive).** The app suggests most things. It hands you a full proposed plan, a full suggested stack, a clear next move, because a lost person needs to be told what to do, not handed an empty page. This is a perfect parent telling a kid who is stuck exactly what to try first.
2. **Subtractive by choice.** Everything it suggests is opt-out-able. You remove what does not fit rather than building up from nothing. Subtracting from a good default is far easier than composing from zero, and it still leaves you in control. This is the "off-switch everywhere" and the floor, applied to the whole plan.
3. **Adaptive on top.** It watches what you keep, what you remove, and your behavioral data, and its next suggestions get better. What you subtract is signal, not just a preference.

The balance: **it leads without forcing.** Confident, prescriptive suggestions for the everyday "what do I do" (because you are lost and it knows more than you right now), and pure depth-pulled restraint for the deep and spiritual and woo layers (which are never suggested until you reach for them). Additive-suggest-then-subtract is the everyday mode; pulled-not-pushed is the depth mode. Both are the perfect parent: it tells you what to do when you are stuck, and it never marches you toward a destination that is not yours.

## Why this also fixes the "too complicated" problem
"Too complicated, tools hard to reach, too much" is a symptom of exposing the whole opinionated system to everyone at once. If the user's goal is the north star and depth is revealed only as pulled, the surface stays calm: the app shows the one or two things that serve this person's current want, not the entire toolbox. **Adaptivity is not only how you respect different users. It is how you make the app simple.** Progressive depth is the UI-simplicity strategy and the philosophy fix in one move. The full toolbox still exists; it just does not all show up at once.

## The read engine (the critical dependency)
The entire spine depends on the app reading the person well enough to adapt: their energy (the energy-door), their goal or lack of one, their belief level (skeptic to seeker), their appetite (how much depth they pull for), and their behavior (what they gravitate to, sustain, or bail on). This is the psychograph from the spiritual-progression spec, generalized to the whole app. It is built from light optional taps plus behavioral signals, never a quiz. **If the read is weak, the adaptation is fake and the app feels generic. The read engine is the highest-value piece of infrastructure to get right.**

## The one risk, and the guard
An app that adapts to everyone can rot into a shapeless everything-tool with no soul. The guard: it must always present **one clear next thing** for this person, never a menu of everything. Focus comes from the adaptation, not from forcing a single path. The soul is the guardian, the tools, and the never-force stance, not a mandated destination.
