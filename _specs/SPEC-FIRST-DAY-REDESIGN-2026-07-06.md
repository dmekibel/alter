## The design laws this flow obeys (from the feedback)
1. Experience first, extract later. The front door asks almost nothing.
2. One real earned win in under a minute. The hook is a felt shift, not a promise.
3. The journey first day is the tutorial. Features are taught by doing them, and the app learns the user by watching, not by quizzing.
4. Tap, do not type. The app is clever enough to offer options.
5. Points are always earned. Press and hold is the charge gesture that earns them.
6. Scientific framing. Placebo is named as a real, usable superpower. Nothing woo up front.
7. Guided audio, natural pauses, breath visuals. Go back everywhere.
8. Evening aware. Never drop a new user into "here is the day you lived."

## PHASE 1: THE FRONT DOOR (target: under 90 seconds, 4 beats)

### Beat 1: The arrival (the hook, no questions)
The guardian mark ignites (halo lights for the first time). Two lines, one at a time, large:

> "I am the part of you that remembers what you actually want."
> "Give me sixty seconds and I will show you something your mind can already do."

One solid tappable control: **Show me.**
No data collected. The promise is a demonstration, not a pitch.

### Beat 2: The first breath (the earned win, felt not told)
Straight into one guided breath, using the breath visual (a circle or wave that expands on the inhale, holds, and falls on the exhale so the user can see when it ends). Guided by voice and text with natural pauses:

> "Breathe in with the light."  (visual expands, ~4s)
> "Hold it a moment."  (~2s)
> "Now let the exhale run longer than the breath in."  (visual falls slowly, ~6s)

Then the guardian names what happened, as earned recognition, not points for nothing:

> "That longer exhale just told your nervous system it is safe. You did that on purpose. That is the whole game: learning to steer your own state."

This single beat delivers the hook, the first real win, the tool style, the breath visual, and the scientific framing at once. No slider, no typing.

### Beat 3: The name (the one acceptable input)
> "What should I call you?"

Name is the only place typing is allowed, because it is unavoidable. Keep it one field, skippable. No gender step (David: it does not matter). If skipped, the guardian addresses the user warmly without a name.

### Beat 4: The one light read (tappable, specific, replaces the ugly slider)
Not a slider, not "where is your charge." One question, specific and warm, with solid tappable chips. Evening aware wording when it is night:

> "How are you arriving right now?"
> [ Wired and can't settle ]  [ Flat and low ]  [ Steady ]  [ Ready to move ]

This is the energy door, redesigned as four specific taps. It routes the journey (low and flat go to a relief-first path, steady and ready go to a build-first path). This is the last thing the front door asks. Everything else is learned by playing.

## PHASE 2: THE JOURNEY AS TUTORIAL (the rest of day one)
The journey first day is a short adaptive path of real tool experiences. Each node teaches a feature by doing it and quietly learns the user. It adapts to the Beat 4 read and to what the user gravitates toward. Text is minimal, big, and revealed a line at a time. Go back is available on every node.

The guardian frames the path once, briefly:

> "We are going to do three small things. Each one leaves something behind that is yours."

### Node 1: The Rewire tool, rebuilt as a guided mantra (see full redesign below)
Teaches: the tools, the sigil charge, and the press-and-hold-earns-points relationship.

### Node 2: Plan tomorrow (tappable, the app offers)
The app does not make the user type tomorrow's one thing. It offers two or three specific options built from what it has already learned (the energy read, the tool they just did, the time), plus a fourth "something else" that reveals a small tappable list, not a text box.

> "One thing for tomorrow. Not a to-do list. One thing that would make tomorrow feel like a win."
> [ Get up before your mind argues ]  [ Ten minutes of the thing you keep avoiding ]  [ One honest conversation ]  [ Something else ]

Teaches: the planner and the timeline, and the idea that the app plans with you. Powerful on day one.

### Node 3: Why this matters (minimal text, high value, evening close)
The "why you are here" content, delivered as one strong idea, not a wall of text. One line at a time, large, with a single visual. Replace the confusing "energy follows clarity, no wrong answers only true ones" framing with something concrete:

> "Most people wait to feel ready. Ready is a feeling you manufacture, not one you wait for."
> "You just manufactured calm with one breath. Tomorrow we build the next piece."

Then the evening handoff (evening aware): the guardian banks the day and points at the morning.

> "It is late. You did the first rep. Come back in the morning and I will show you how to start your day before the day starts you."

If it is not evening, Node 3 instead opens the morning path directly rather than handing off to sleep.

## THE REWIRE TOOL, REDESIGNED (guided audio mantra, not press next)

### Frame it scientifically first (two lines, big)
> "This is mental rehearsal. Athletes and surgeons use it because it works: your brain barely separates a vividly imagined rep from a real one."
> "We are going to install one line. Say it like you mean it and your brain files it as evidence."

### Offer tapping, optional, explained
> "Want to add tapping? Lightly tapping these points while you speak is a way to keep your body calm while the line goes in. It is not magic. It is you, using placebo on purpose, which is a real edge."
> [ Add tapping ]   [ Just the words ]

### The mantra: guided, specific, spoken, paused
The app tells the user exactly what to say. Lines are specific and concrete, not short abstract slogans. Voice plus text, natural pauses, breath visual where it breathes. Press and hold to charge the sigil while speaking (this earns the points).

Proposed line set (concrete, present tense, not corny):

> "Say this out loud, and hold the mark while you do." (press and hold begins, sigil starts to charge)
> "I move before the fear finishes its sentence."  (pause)
> "When it is hard, that is my signal to begin, not to stop."  (pause)
> "I am the person who does the thing anyway."  (release, the sigil locks in with a felt confirmation)

Then earned recognition, not a generic next:

> "That line is now charged. You will see this mark again tomorrow. Each time you say it, it gets faster, until one day you do not need to say it at all."

Notes:
- Breath visual whenever a breath is cued. Go back available throughout.
- The press-and-hold charge is the points source. Points are earned by the rep, never by a tap that does nothing.
- The specific lines above are placeholders to react to. The rule: concrete and scene based, never "picture yourself being the way you want to be."

## CROSS-CUTTING COMPONENTS THIS FLOW REQUIRES (build once, reuse everywhere)
1. **Guided audio engine with natural pauses.** Plays a scripted sequence of spoken lines with tuned pauses, synced to on-screen text that reveals one line at a time. Uses the existing Web Audio engine ([[alter-voice-web-audio]]).
2. **Breath visual.** A circle or wave that expands on inhale, holds, and falls on exhale, timed so the user can see when the exhale ends. Reused by every breathing cue in the app.
3. **Universal go back.** Every ritual and node can step backward without losing state.
4. **Press-and-hold charge.** The gesture that charges a sigil and earns points, shared by the rewire tool and future rituals (see `SPEC-RITUAL-ENGINE`).
