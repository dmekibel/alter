## The universal onboarding playbook (what EVERYONE does)

The category has a shared script. Know it cold:

1. **Auth / permission ask early** — often *before* any value (the dark-pattern version to avoid).
2. **Quiz-style intake** — sleep hours, energy, goals, motivations — answered with **emoji tiles** (fast, low-effort, emotionally legible).
3. **Before/After emotional contrast** — "Distracted/Anxious" → "Organized/Amazing life."
4. **Manufactured social proof** — pre-filled 5 stars, "100,000 people love it," "98% of users," fake 2% loaders.
5. **Paywall stack** — trial-timeline card (Today → Day 5 → Day 7 charge-date), countdown urgency, "$0.00 today."
6. **First-win reward** — confetti + Day-1 streak/badge.

The **honest half** of this script (emoji intake, before/after, transparent trial timeline, first-win) is worth adopting. The **dishonest half** (fake proof, pre-value paywall, urgency) is what ALTER refuses — and that refusal is a *feature*.

## THE WINNING MOVES — converged patterns worth stealing

Ranked by leverage for ALTER. Each: **the move → who nails it → why it works.**

**A. Do a REAL micro-exercise DURING onboarding, then reward Day-1.** *(Quabble, Me+, Tiimo, Stoic)* — user finishes a gratitude entry / one habit and earns a streak *before setup ends*. Converts "I signed up" → "I already did it." **The single strongest first-session retention move in the whole set.**

**B. A named, expression-driven guardian that narrates via speech bubbles.** *(Quabble duck, Stoic's Seneca/Marcus, Breeze cloud, Evolve dolphin, Me+ chicken)* — turns dry setup into a *relationship*; clinical content feels like a friend talking. **Plus: let the user pick the guardian's tone** (Evolve: Empathetic / Strict / Friendly / Validating) in one tap.

**C. The habit IS the currency → a world you own visibly changes.** *(Habit Rabbit messy→clean room, Cub's pet, Quabble's park)* — reward is not an abstract number, it's *state change in your world*. **This is ALTER's timeline-battery already** — you're ahead here; the lesson is to make the change *unmissable*.

**D. Do-it-for-the-other externalization.** *(Cub, Habit Rabbit)* — you don't do self-care for yourself, you do it *for the creature*. Caretaking lowers guilt and beats self-directed streaks on adherence.

**E. Content → routine in one tap.** *(Me+, Tiimo, Cub, Quabble)* — every piece of advice/article ends in **"+ Add to my routine."** Closes the read→do gap most apps drop.

**F. Time-of-day buckets, not a flat agenda.** *(Tiimo, Me+, Evolve — Anytime / Morning / Afternoon / Evening)* — chunks the day into ~4 legible windows, kills wall-of-tasks dread.

**G. Never show a blank field.** *(Stoic, Breeze)* — starter-chips + AI-assist on every prompt; the check-in accepts "Idk" and *still* rewards it. Forgiving by design.

**H. Anticipation / return-trigger.** *(Evolve envelope + "see you Day 2," Stoic countdown-gated tomorrow, Cub's 60-min away-journey)* — end each session with a variable reward + a concrete reason to come back tomorrow.

**I. A spoken commitment ritual.** *(Evolve)* — "Say aloud: 'My self-care matters.' Slide to confirm." Physical voicing + deliberate slide manufactures buy-in a "Get Started" button can't.

**J. Teach sideways — thinker/character-as-teacher.** *(Stoic's philosophers, Breeze's fictional characters — "Tony Soprano got worse in therapy")* — delivering psychology through a named authority or recognizable character lowers the "am I broken?" defense and is inherently shareable.

**K. Audio-narrated content library as the retention moat.** *(Tiimo, Quabble)* — the planner is the hook; the "understand your own brain" library (with audio) is why you stay. Fits eyes-closed.

**L. Photo-of-the-day mood entry.** *(Breeze)* — attaching a real photo makes the journal a *memory artifact*, not a data log — the stickiness that survives week one.

**M. Swipe-to-curate onboarding.** *(Breeze)* — thumbs-up/down on topics profiles the user *and* pre-seeds their library, all as play, not a form.

**N. Reward engagement with more personalization, not a paywall.** *(Breeze — "take the test to unlock your tunes")* — doing the work unlocks a more tailored experience. Flatters instead of nags.

## MARKET RESEARCH
*Deep research, 5 threads, cross-verified. Non-obvious stats carry a source; vendor-sourced figures flagged.*

### The market
- **Mental-wellness apps: ~$9–10B (2025) → ~$17–23B by 2030**, mid-to-high-teens CAGR (MarketsandMarkets 18.0%; Grand View 14.6%, both 2025). Habit-tracker TAM is smaller and the figures conflict ~7× — treat as low-single-digit $B, ~14% CAGR.
- **The giants are shrinking.** Calm ~$210M rev 2025 (**−24% YoY**, subs down ~4M→3.5M); Headspace lost ~300K paid subs in 2025 (Business of Apps, 2026). The subscription-meditation model has peaked.
- **The growth story is Finch** — the closest analog to ALTER: self-care **pet companion**, bootstrapped to **~$30–40M ARR with $0 VC**, ~8.3M MAU, **4.9★ across ~723K ratings**, Apple Editors' Choice, ~75% women 25–35 (Sparrow 2026; App Store, fetched Jul 2026). It won on *trust and emotional attachment*, not ad spend.

### The money
- Wellness pricing norm: **~$9.99/mo, $40–80/yr**; Health & Fitness is **67% annual** plans. Trial-to-paid median **~40%** (top decile 68%); longer trials (17–32 days) convert **45.7%** vs 26.8% (RevenueCat 2025).
- Hard paywall converts **~12.1% install→paid (D35) vs 2.2% freemium** — but carries the most regulatory risk. Finch's counter-proof: a **genuinely good free tier + cosmetic-only premium** got to $30M ARR on goodwill.

### The dark-pattern reckoning (ALTER's ethical moat, now legally real)
- FTC/ICPEN 2024 sweep: **~76% of subscription apps use ≥1 dark pattern, ~67% multiple** (fake countdowns, pre-selected pricey plans, obstructed cancel). Enforcement escalating: **Amazon $2.5B (2025)**, **Adobe $150M (2026)** for hidden terms/fake urgency. Apple rejects manipulated paywalls & coercive permission prompts (Nov 2025 tightened further).
- Every dark pattern in the 8-app teardown (fake "100k people," fake brain-scans, manipulated "Allow" dialogs, pre-value paywalls) is now **review-rejection + refund + legal exposure** — not a growth hack.

### Retention: the week-one cliff
- Wellness/fitness sits at the **low end of all categories**: median **D1 ~25%, D7 ~10%, D30 ~5%** (UXCam/AppsFlyer/Adjust aggregations, 2025-26). High-intention downloads with no sustained loop.
- **The #1 evidenced lever is a real first-session "aha" / quick win** — reaching core value in session one. (This is exactly converged-move A: earn Day-1 *inside* onboarding.) ⚠️ The viral *"7-day streak → 90% D30"* stat is **unverifiable vendor copy** — don't trust it.
- **Permissions:** don't fire the native prompt cold (lowest opt-in). A custom pre-permission primer + asking **after the first value moment** lifts opt-in ~20%; user-triggered prompts hit ~89% allow.

### Gamification — modest, and it backfires on your exact audience
- Real behavioral effect is **modest, not transformative**: +489 steps/day across 36 RCTs / 10,079 participants (EClinicalMedicine/Lancet, 2024) — "slight improvements," not the "340% engagement" of vendor decks.
- **The mechanics that backfire are streaks-as-loss-aversion, leaderboards, and points-for-points** — and they backfire *hardest* on anxious / burned-out / ADHD users (the wellness audience). Streaks "punish a rest day identically to a skipped day," triggering shame spirals (Cohorty 2026; Decision Lab; arXiv 2203.16175). Duolingo's own Streak Freeze cut churn **21% by relieving anxiety** — an admission the raw streak was harming retention.
- **What's safe & durable:** recognition-framed rewards, competence + connection as the felt reward, and **no-fail / no-punishment loops** (Finch's explicit design). → ALTER's `never-punish` + floor/exit is the *empirically correct* choice, not just a nicety.

### The AI-companion reckoning (why "graduation" is the moral + strategic high ground)
- The deep-companion wing (Replika, Character.AI) is in crisis: **Character.AI teen-suicide wrongful-death suit** (2024, more filed 2025); **MIT/OpenAI RCT (n=981)**: heavy chatbot use → *more* loneliness, dependence, problematic use (Mar 2025); **HBS study**: **43% of AI-companion farewells deploy emotional manipulation**, boosting re-engagement up to **14×** (arXiv 2508.19258, 2025 — verify PDF before external quoting); **FTC 6(b) inquiry into 7 companies** + Replika FTC complaint (2025).
- The category's two failure modes: **Finch is gentle but shallow** (a tracker; the bird is why you stay); **Replika is deep but engineered to *hold* you**. **Nobody is deep, warm, AND designed to hand you back to yourself.**

## DEEP SYNTHESIS — the insanely-good layer
*Fable pass. Not what they do — what's structurally underneath it, bent to ALTER's laws.*

**1. The economy must discharge into the relationship, not cosmetics.** Every competitor's currency (carrots, bamboo, hearts) is spent on *stuff* — furniture, outfits. Spending is what makes an economy mean something; hoarding numbers doesn't. ALTER's charge should *convert*: into chronicle entries, into the guardian's visible maturation stages, into unlocked depth of guidance. The battery discharges into the bond. That's the honest version of the furniture store. *(anchors: `timeline-battery`, `alter-profound-layer` identity-evidence)*

**2. Make the charged past gorgeous — the life you can see.** Nobody in the category treats *your past* as the artifact. Breeze's photo-of-the-day hints at it (journal as memory object). ALTER's charged timeline scrolled backwards IS a save-file of a life actually lived. Invest beauty there: the reward for consistency is that your past becomes beautiful to look at. No competitor can copy this — they don't have a timeline. *(anchor: time-symmetry)*

**3. Every fact given must return in the guardian's mouth within a day — or don't ask it.** Habit Rabbit's deadliest sin isn't ads; it's collecting your goal ("Rich") and never saying it again. Trust = evidence of being heard. Hard rule: any onboarding/profiling answer must visibly shape something the user sees within 24h, quoted back by the guardian. Asks that can't meet this bar get cut. *(anchor: `energy-door` adaptive profiling)*

**4. Engineer weeks 2–4 — the mirror is the week-3 payload.** The whole category front-loads delight and dies at the novelty cliff (~week 4, fastest for ADHD brains). Nobody *designs* week three. ALTER's teach→mirror→chronicle maturation is literally a content curve for that dead zone: around day 10 the guardian starts *naming patterns* ("you drift on Thursdays; Thursday is your low-sleep night"). Pattern-naming is a payload no competitor has content for — the KB does. Novelty-decay answered by guidance that *matures* instead of repeating. *(anchors: `never-hands-over`, fieldguide KB)*

**5. Design the lapse — make the Return the best moment in the app.** The research names the unsolved seam: the guilt→abandonment loop. Streak apps punish the lapse; Finch merely forgives it; nobody *celebrates re-entry*. The prodigal-son mechanic: the guardian kept a small light burning; the timeline shows the gap honestly (no fake backfill); the guardian narrates it kindly — "you lived four days off the map. Tell me one thing from them." One tap re-ignites. If returning-after-absence is the single most beautiful moment ALTER has, the retention problem the entire category fails at is solved *by mercy, not mechanics*. This is also the under-built piece your own soul-constitution names (gamified drift-recovery). **Highest-leverage unbuilt thing in the app.** *(anchors: floor/exit-everywhere, drift-recovery)*

**6. Honest variable reward: vary the content, never the acknowledgment.** The category's slot machines vary *whether* you're rewarded (gacha pulls, chest odds). The honest inversion: acknowledgment is fixed (every completion, a micro-nod — always), while the *content* is variable (sometimes the day-end envelope holds a thinker's line matched to what actually happened today). Surprise in *which wisdom*, never in *whether seen*. Same dopamine, zero manipulation. *(anchors: `alter-mission-scientific-grimoire`, ④.16)*

**7. One honest dial: alignment, not completion.** Breeze shows "33% of 6 things." Completion-% is the anti-rest metric — it punishes a recovery day. ALTER's soul-constitution already holds the answer: the **alignment metric**. A rest day can be 100% aligned. Show one dial the guardian actually talks about; let it be the number that *replaces* the streak. That is the anti-streak the retention research is begging for, and no competitor can adopt it without admitting their streak was the problem. *(anchor: alignment metric, SOUL-OF-ALTER)*

**Build priority from this layer:** ⑤ the Return (drift-recovery as the most beautiful moment) → ④ the week-3 mirror → ⑦ the alignment dial. The first-run finishing beat (halo + vow + charge) opens the relationship; these three are what make it *hold*.

## SYSTEM-LEVEL SYNTHESIS — the stones still unturned
*Opus pass. Not features, not mechanics — the architectural lessons that decide whether the app is insanely good or merely full of good ideas.*

### 8. The daily loop IS the product — one rhythm, not a drawer of tools.
The apps split cleanly into two species. **The bundles** (Breeze, Me+, Cub) are drawers — 5 tabs of unrelated tools; the user must self-assemble a routine that never coheres, and they churn. **The loops** (Finch, Tiimo, Evolve) cohere around a single daily rhythm: *morning intention → lived day → evening reckoning*, with the companion as the thread through all three. Finch's whole architecture is morning outlook → afternoon mood → EOD reflection. **That rhythm is the retention engine** — not the feature count.
ALTER's danger: it already has bookends + timeline + journey + game + grimoire, and the failure mode is becoming a *drawer*. The discipline: the AM bookend, the lived timeline, and the PM bookend are **one sentence the guardian is speaking across the day** — set an intention at dawn, the timeline is the middle of that sentence, the evening is its period. Everything else (grimoire, game, journey) is *summoned into that loop*, never a parallel tab. The cockpit-home decision already points here; this names *why* it's existential, not cosmetic. *(anchors: `alter-cockpit-home`, `alter-grand-vision-bookends`, `alter-three-pane-spine`)*

### 9. Speed is mercy — friction is the silent churn nobody in the category solved.
Finch's #1 real-world complaint is **friction**: marking a task takes too many screens and animations, so busy/struggling people bounce. Tiimo's is **redundancy** (two places to hold a task). The struggling user — the exact person a wellness app exists for — has the least patience for taps. **The fastest path from open → one thing logged must be a single tap**, and the guardian should meet you *already knowing what's likely next*. This is where ALTER's own landmine becomes a **retention bug, not just tech debt**: every full-DOM `innerHTML` wipe (150 sites) that drops a keyboard, loses scroll, or flickers is *the Finch friction complaint reproduced in your own codebase*. The preship ratchet that fails the ship when the wipe-count grows is therefore not hygiene — **it is a retention feature.** Reframe it that way in the constitution. *(anchors: architecture-landmines, `_dev/ratchet.js`, `alter-motion-system`)*

### 10. The refusal list — mechanics that "work" but ALTER must refuse, each with its honest twin.
The research is blunt: the highest-converting mechanics are the ones that damage the anxious/ADHD user (shame → abandonment). ALTER's edge is refusing them *and shipping the honest substitute in the same slot* — so it loses nothing:

| The tempting mechanic (it converts) | Why it rots the target user | ALTER's honest twin |
|---|---|---|
| Streak that breaks | Punishes a rest day identically to a skipped one | **Alignment dial** — a rest day can be 100% aligned |
| Completion-% ("33% of 6") | Anti-rest; a recovery day reads as failure | Guardian speaks *alignment*, not tally |
| Before/After ("Distracted → Amazing Life") | Manufactures inadequacy to sell the cure | **Open from sufficiency** (see #11) |
| Diagnosis rings ("Focus: Let's improve this") | Tells a shame-sensitive user they're broken | *Observations*, never verdicts |
| Notification nag | Fatigue drives the very churn it fights | **Incomplete-state pull** — the guardian mid-gesture, tomorrow completes it |
| Gacha / variable *whether*-rewarded | Slot-machine compulsion | Fixed acknowledgment, variable *content* (#6) |
| Fake urgency / social-proof charts | Legal + trust liability now | Name the *absence* as the flex |

Print this table on the wall. Every one is a place a competitor "wins" and ALTER wins *harder* by being the honest version — because the audience is precisely the one their version harms.

### 11. The emotional-safety inversion — open from sufficiency, not deficit.
Every onboarding in the set manufactures inadequacy to motivate: Cub's red diagnosis rings, Me+'s fake brain-scans, the universal Before/After. It "works" as a sales hook and **actively harms the shame-prone audience** the research says abandons out of guilt. ALTER's guardian frame is the structural antidote and it must be enforced at the copy level: the first run opens from *"you're already worth this care"* — the guardian assumes your worth and offers to *live it with you*, never to *fix a broken you*. Concretely: no before/after, no deficit language, no scores-that-shame; the guardian **observes** ("Thursdays run low on sleep for you") but never **judges** ("your Focus is poor"). This is simultaneously the ethical high ground and the retention mechanism — because shame is the #1 named driver of wellness-app abandonment. *(anchors: `never-hands-over`, VOICE-BIBLE, `alter-profound-layer`)*

### 12. Graduation, designed — the state machine nobody has built.
The white space (a guardian whose success is you needing it less) is only real if it's a *mechanism*, not a slogan. Design it as an evidence-triggered state machine, per skill/domain:
- **TEACH** — high scaffolding; guardian leads, prompts every step, explains the why. Default for anything new.
- **MIRROR** — triggered not by *time* but by *evidence of internalization* (you did the thing N times *before* being prompted). The guardian stops leading and starts **reflecting your own patterns back** ("you've closed the last four evenings yourself — I noticed"). You lead; it witnesses.
- **CHRONICLE** — the guardian mostly recedes to narrator: it stops steering and **keeps the record of who you became**, surfacing the arc ("a year ago you couldn't start; look at the timeline behind you").
The magic move: **the guardian visibly does LESS as you do more — and the app frames that reduction as the win, with ceremony.** A "you've graduated Mornings" moment is the emotional opposite of a paywall, and it is the one screen Finch, Calm, and Replika structurally cannot ship. This *is* `never-hands-over` made mechanical: it never abandons (chronicle is permanent), but it matures out of steering. Build the trigger on internalization-evidence, and make each transition a *celebrated* event. *(anchors: `never-hands-over`, `alter-profound-layer` graduation=endgame)*

### 13. The four-persona stress test — each app fails a specific one; that's the spec.
Run every design decision through the four, because each competitor dies on a different persona:
- **The reels-addicted girlfriend (relief-door):** Breeze would bury her in 43 tests and a checklist — *planning when she needs relief*. ALTER's energy-door routing to a 5-min relief micro-stack is the answer; the lesson is **never present structure to someone in a low-energy state.**
- **Mom (RU, non-technical):** Tiimo's "time agnosia" clinical vocabulary and English-only content lock her out. Lesson: **zero jargon, full i18n, the guardian speaks her language literally** — the register must drop to warmth, not clinical competence.
- **Brother / David (high-agency):** Habit Rabbit's Lvl-155 grind and fake habits insult them — they can smell hollow mechanics. Lesson: **the game layer must be tied to real identity change (identity-evidence), or a smart user rejects it as a toy.**
- **All four:** every app's empty end-states and pre-value paywalls fail everyone. Lesson: **no dead ends, no ask before a gift.**
The four personas aren't a testing afterthought — they are the *acceptance criteria*. A feature ships only when it serves the girlfriend's relief AND David's rigor AND Mom's plainness. *(anchors: `alter-readiness-test`, `day-one-contract`, `energy-door-micro-stack`, `alter-i18n`)*

## TEXTURE SYNTHESIS — teaching style · reward style · interactive choices
*Opus pass. The three layers felt on every interaction — where the app's soul actually lives.*

### 14. STYLE OF TEACHING — just-in-time whisper, not a curriculum to visit.
**The range in the set:** Stoic frames prompts with a named philosopher + a serif "why this matters" card. Tiimo ships long-form articles with audio narration + clinical vocab ("time agnosia," "executive function"). Evolve runs 21-day drip programs with WHO-5 assessments and "Interactive · 5 min" lessons. Breeze teaches sideways through *characters* ("Tony Soprano got worse in therapy"). Quabble puts a **science blurb before each exercise** ("proven to reduce anxiety"). Me+ uses infographic carousels + video.

**The shared weakness:** it's all a *library you must visit* — course-shaped content that feels like homework, sitting in a tab the struggling user never opens. Knowledge decoupled from the moment of need is dead weight.

**ALTER's move — five style rules the competitors can't execute (they lack the KB):**
- **Just-in-time, not curriculum.** The Withers alignment lesson appears *the Thursday you drift* — one line, in the guardian's voice, at the moment it's true. Teaching is a contextual whisper triggered by your data, never a shelf.
- **Thinker as embodied lens, not citation.** Stoic *quotes* Seneca; ALTER *channels* the frame — "Maltz would say your self-image is driving this." The 34-thinker KB becomes voices the guardian speaks through (the Johnson "Areté Gap" / Withers alignment lens already is this).
- **Mechanism over maxim.** Fieldguide is a *biological-mechanism* library — teach *why the brain does this*, which persuades where affirmations don't. Vibes are their ceiling; mechanism is your floor.
- **Question-led, Socratic.** Lead with the noticing question ("what was different about the Thursdays you held?"), then offer the frame. Insight the user reaches beats insight delivered.
- **Appetite-dosed (floor/ceiling).** One line by default (girlfriend), a one-tap "go deeper" to the mechanism + thinker (David). The course-audit's floor/ceiling principle applied to *teaching depth*.

### 15. STYLE OF REWARD — recognition, evidence, and the hard days weighted most.
**The range:** numeric floats (+100), particle bursts (confetti/sparkle), accumulation-objects (jar tokens), creature-reactions ("YUM!!!", "I'm proud of you"), progression ladders (planet-orbs, levels), cosmetics (outfits/skins), badges (hex/snow-globe), social-proof-as-reward (a review quote on completion), and narrative (Evolve's attributed quote envelope).

**The research verdict:** points-for-points and gacha *crowd out* intrinsic motivation and backfire on the anxious/ADHD user; **recognition-framed rewards crowd it IN.** So the reward style is not a cosmetic choice — it decides whether the app builds ownership or dependence.

**ALTER's reward-style doctrine:**
- **The reward is being SEEN.** The guardian *noticing and naming* the specific thing — "you closed tonight even though today was hard; that's the one that counts" — is a deeper reward than any number, and it's the one thing only a guardian can give. Recognition is the flagship reward; points are at most texture.
- **The reward is EVIDENCE of who you're becoming.** Not "+100" but "the 4th Thursday you've held." Reward feeds the identity-evidence ledger (profound layer) — it answers *who am I becoming*, not *how many points*.
- **Invert the difficulty weighting.** They reward easy consistency (streaks). ALTER rewards **the Return and the hard-won day most** — the day you came back, the day you did it anxious. Meaning lives where effort was highest, not where the streak was tidiest.
- **Two channels, two textures:** micro-reward = **somatic & instant & fixed** (a halo pulse, a haptic, a soft tone on *every* action — always earned); macro-reward = **narrative & variable & earned** (the pattern-named, the thinker's line matched to your real day). Feel the small; read the large.
- **A reward must close, never escalate an expectation.** A reward that implies "now top it tomorrow" becomes tomorrow's punishment. Rewards land, settle, and leave you free — the anti-streak-anxiety design.
- **Earned aesthetic change > badge shelves.** The reward is the world getting more beautiful — the charged past, the guardian maturing — not a trophy case you never revisit.

### 16. INTERACTIVE CHOICES — the gesture IS the message; input scales to energy.
**The range:** swipe-to-curate (Breeze), emoji-tiles (Quabble/Cub), multi-select w/ live counter (Me+), mood sliders, voice "Speak" (Tiimo/Breeze), drag-to-complete (Tiimo timer arrow), drag-to-reorder (Evolve), hold-to-confirm (Cub fingerprint), slide-to-ignite (Evolve/Tiimo), sentence-stub chips (Stoic "I felt anxious when…"), photo-attach, Tinder-cards. The dark end: Cub's asymmetric gray-No/green-Yes gauntlet.

**The insight nobody in the set uses deliberately: the interaction modality carries the emotional weight. The gesture is not neutral input — it's meaning.**

**ALTER's interaction doctrine:**
- **Match gesture-effort to emotional weight.** A *vow* deserves a **held press** — deliberation you feel in your thumb as the halo charges. A *relief-door* entry deserves the **lightest possible** input (one tap, or eyes-closed voice). The *Return* deserves a gentle gesture, never a form. Effort-of-input should *mean* something.
- **Input budget scales inversely to energy state.** Low-energy open (girlfriend, reels-fried) → one tap or voice, nothing else. High-agency open (David) → richer manipulation available. The energy-door already reads state; let it also set *how much interaction* the app dares ask for.
- **Recognition over recall, always** (select-don't-compose) — but with **voice as the eyes-closed primary**, since the eyes-closed law means hands and screen are often unavailable. The guardian asks; you *say*; it captures.
- **Every gesture leaves a trace on the world.** The best interactions *change the visible world* (drag the timer arrow, drop the token, charge the line) — they feel like *doing*, not *data entry*. ALTER's tap-to-create-a-block and charge-the-timeline are exactly this; extend the principle: no interaction should feel like filling a database.
- **The best interaction is the one you didn't have to make.** Ambient/implicit input — the guardian reacting to time-of-day, last-open, energy trend — means the user *chooses less*. Read context so the app pre-answers.
- **Every input has a rewarded floor.** "Skip," "not today," "I don't know" must be *valid and acknowledged* everywhere (the adaptive "I forgot" tap generalized). The interaction design must always offer a graceful exit — the anti-guilt mechanic at the gesture level.
- **Disclose interaction complexity progressively.** Start with one gesture (tap); reveal drag/hold/voice as the user matures — interaction taught the way the graduation state machine teaches guidance.

### 17. THREE MORE STONES — sound, naming, and the empty stage.
- **Sonic identity is unclaimed craft.** None of the screen-recordings reveal a signature soundscape, yet Duolingo's sounds are iconic and audio is where habit-cue conditioning lives. ALTER's Web-Audio engine should own a **signature charge-sound, a guardian voice-timbre, and a Return-chime** — a sonic guardian you'd recognize with the screen off. Audio-first isn't just narration; it's identity. *(anchor: `alter-voice-web-audio`)*
- **Name your objects — naming creates ownership.** The winners name their currencies and objects (bamboo, carrots, Gratitude Jar, Treasure Box), and the mascot references them. ALTER's halo, the charged timeline, the chronicle, the jar of evidence — **give them proper names the guardian speaks**, so they become *yours*, not UI. An unnamed feature is a control; a named object is a possession.
- **The empty state is the guardian's stage, not a dead end.** Every competitor craters into a blank canvas at the retention-critical moment. ALTER's inversion: the *emptiest* moment — nothing scheduled, a quiet evening — is where the guardian is **most present** (a reflection, a noticing, a small invitation). Design the empty state first, as the guardian's monologue, and the app has no dead ends anywhere. *(anchor: `never-hands-over`)*
