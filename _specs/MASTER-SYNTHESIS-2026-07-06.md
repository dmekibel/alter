ALTER's defensible position is the one seam every funded competitor is *structurally forbidden* from occupying: **a guardian that never punishes a lapse and never abandons you — whose success is measured by you needing its guidance less, even as you use its timeless tools forever.** Around that: honest versions of the best engagement mechanics in mobile (June's Journey's investment loop, the collector's pride) pointed at something *real* (a life actually lived), monetized without a single dark pattern, and delivered as *interactive rituals that measurably program the user's own brain.*

## The revelation ledger (nothing swept under the rug)
Every non-obvious thing this session surfaced, in one list:

**On the competition:**
1. The category = engagement mechanics wearing a wellness skin; monetization is the product.
2. Universal sins (paywall-before-value, fake proof, dark patterns, feature sprawl, two design languages, empty end-states) are now **legal/review liability** (FTC 76%; Amazon $2.5B; Adobe $150M).
3. Finch ($30–40M ARR, bootstrapped, 4.9★) proves **honest wins**; Calm/Headspace are shrinking.
4. Retention is a week-one cliff (D30 ~5%); the #1 lever is a real **first-session win**.
5. Gamification's real effect is **modest**, and streaks **backfire** on the anxious/ADHD audience.
6. The AI-companion wing is in a **legal reckoning** for engineering dependence (Character.AI suit; MIT RCT; HBS 43%/14×; FTC 6(b)).
7. **~2% of wellness apps have any evidence; ZERO are built for graduation** — that white space is uncontested (Life Note is the only near-competitor on "grounded thinkers," and it's shallow).

**On ALTER's design:**
8. The economy must **discharge into the relationship** (not cosmetics-for-their-own-sake); the charged **past** should be made gorgeous (a save-file of a life lived).
9. **Every fact given must return in the guardian's mouth within 24h — or don't ask it.**
10. **Engineer weeks 2–4** — the week-3 "mirror" (pattern-naming from the KB) is the payload no rival can build.
11. **The Return** (drift-recovery as the most beautiful moment in the app) is the highest-leverage *unbuilt* thing — it solves the guilt→abandonment loop the whole research corpus names as unsolved.
12. **Honest variable reward:** fix the acknowledgment, vary the *content* (which thinker's line), never the *whether*.
13. **One dial: alignment, not completion** (a rest day can be 100% aligned) — the anti-streak metric.
14. **Speed is mercy** — the innerHTML-wipe debt is a *retention bug*, not just tech debt; the ratchet is a retention feature.
15. **Open from sufficiency, not deficit** — no before/after shaming; observations, never diagnoses (shame drives abandonment).
16. **Graduation is a state machine:** Teach → Mirror (triggered by evidence of internalization) → Chronicle; the guardian visibly does less as you do more, and *ceremonializes that reduction as the win.*
17. **The four personas are the acceptance criteria** — a feature ships only when it serves the girlfriend's relief AND David's rigor AND Mom's plainness AND the brother.
18. **Teaching style:** just-in-time whisper, not a curriculum; thinker-as-embodied-lens; mechanism over maxim; Socratic; appetite-dosed.
19. **Reward style:** recognition ("being seen") > points; reward is *evidence of who you're becoming*; **invert the difficulty weighting** (reward the hard-won day and the Return most).
20. **Interaction:** the gesture IS the message; input effort scales to emotional weight; input budget scales *inversely* to energy state; voice as the eyes-closed primary; every input has a rewarded floor.
21. **Sound identity, named objects, and the empty-state-as-the-guardian's-stage** are unclaimed craft.

**On monetization (David WILL charge):**
22. Free buys the transformation; paid buys **expression, depth, and a guardian that knows you** — never growth or access. Refusal list holds.

**Corrections David issued (load-bearing):**
23. ALTER is **used forever** — tools/planner/timer are timeless; graduation ≠ abandonment, only the *guidance* matures.
24. The Sechenov files are out of scope.
