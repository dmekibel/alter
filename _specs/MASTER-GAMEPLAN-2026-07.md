## REVISION 2026-08-15 — THE USABILITY & DEPTH ROADMAP (David's four phases, spoken this session)

The 2c home surface is finally 1:1 with his Claude Design frame and device-confirmed (see `DECISIONS.md` 2026-08-15).
With the design saga closed, David set the forward order himself. Captured here verbatim in intent, with my sequencing
notes. **This is a DEPTH roadmap, not a launch roadmap — see the tension named at the bottom, which needs his verdict.**

### PHASE 1 — MAKE IT TRUSTWORTHY (the usability floor)
*"get rid of all the basic bugs that make the app unusable or confusing"*

1. **Popups.** Old, fire randomly, sometimes break the app. His instinct: rethink all of them, likely delete and remake.
2. **Never land at the top of the journey.** Garden → home, or swiping left out of the garden, dumps you at the trail's
   very beginning. It should always land at the journey's live end.
3. **The breathing tool** (his biggest tools complaint, attempted several times): switchable visual; an explicit
   inhale/hold/exhale indicator; a toggleable cue sound with a DIFFERENT sound per phase and selectable sets (bell /
   gong / gentle woodblocks); and a toggleable guiding tone whose pitch rises on the inhale and falls on the exhale —
   gentle, never ugly. A version of that tone exists and "sounds really bad."
4. **Voice switching is broken.** Male ↔ female does nothing until the app is closed and reopened.
5. **The meditation story-bar.** In a stack the IG-story bar is right; a multi-part meditation expands its own bar and
   reads as confusing. Proposal: always ONE bar per top-level step, express the meditation's parts somewhere secondary.

6. **The pause / timing engine.** *"Regardless of which tool, the pauses are all identical and kind of too slow."* His
   diagnosis is not that one number is mis-tuned but that the app has ONE pause model where it needs several, and that the
   GRANULARITY is wrong: instructional/somatic cues want a short beat after EVERY line ("relax your eyebrows, pause, relax
   your eyes, pause"), while contemplative and visualization prompts want a real dwell. Today it appears to batch lines and
   then insert one long gap, which reads as out of place in the stretch stack. His words: *"pausing and timing is a very
   important aspect of tools that has to be very thought out."* Likely BOTH engine work (a pause-context model) and content
   work (authoring which line gets which context).

Root causes, anchors and costs: six parallel investigations were run this session; their findings fill this section.
**Sequencing note:** popups first — they are the only category that BREAKS the app, and they can fire during any other
test, so fixing them makes every later verification trustworthy. Then the two contained bugs (voice, journey landing).
Breathing earns its own session (it is a phase engine + a sound design, with real product choices inside).

### PHASE 2 — THE GARDEN + THE FIVE-WAY WORLD
*"slightly adjusting the garden the way we set it up"*

1. **Empty states.** Every garden menu except the store is empty until you add something, and reads as broken. Each menu
   should teach what it is, and offer the fastest possible first action. **Claude Design work.**
2. **Plant anywhere there is space** — replacing the pick-one-of-three fixed slots with real placement against the
   island's occupancy map. **Engineering.**
3. **The gardener WALKS to the spot and plants it** — no magic planting. Default interaction becomes tap-a-spot-and-he-
   walks; the joystick survives as a setting because he likes it. **The biggest build of the three; it is what turns
   planting from a menu action into something alive.**
4. **Bring back the horizontal swipes:** left → planner, right → garden. Resolved his own blocker: garden panning
   becomes TWO fingers so ONE finger can be the pane swipe.
5. **Overshoot must be impossible** in every direction. *(Vertical half already shipped v1288/v1298 and is gate-locked;
   the horizontal half arrives with the swipes.)*
6. **Pane transition animations + a DIRECTIONAL home cascade**: the board assembles from the direction you arrived from —
   top-down from the journey, bottom-up from the tools, and left/right from the planner/garden. **Designed in Claude
   Design first**; prompt drafted at `_design-sync/PROMPT-pane-transitions.md`.
