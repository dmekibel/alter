# THE VOICE BIBLE — the law for every line ALTER speaks or shows
*2026-07-03, standing artifact (local only). Synthesized from the tools-1000x design tournament (3 proposals + 3 judges, `_specs/_tournament-tools-1000x-2026-07-03/`). This is the lint for ALL future copy: tool cards, pools, toasts, onboarding, journey. A line that violates it does not ship.*

## HARD RULES, 2026-07-07 UPDATE (top priority, from David's hands-on review)
These come first. They override any sample line below that predates them. **Every line also passes `_specs/WRITING-SYSTEM.md` first** (the anti-AI-voice gate: specificity over abstraction; ban the "X, not Y" negation-reversal, slop words, template openers, the wrap-up bow; the anti-template test).
- **No em-dashes in any screen or written text.** They read as AI. Use commas (already "breaths" in this doc), periods, colons, or a line break for a pause. Note: some existing spoken clips and protected KEEPERS use a dash as a breath marker in their recorded source. Those are grandfathered as audio cadence, but when a keeper renders as on-screen text, the visual dash becomes a comma. Any brand-new line uses no dash at all.
- **No vague language.** Vagueness reads as AI. "Where is your charge right now" is dead; "your energy level" or a concrete state lives. Every line is specific, thoughtful, meaningful. Fewer words is not the goal; precision is.
- **No corny, oversimplified, or abstract lines.** "Picture yourself already being the way you want to be" is abstract and dies. "Picture it already true, 3, 2, 1" is corny and dies. Use specific, concrete, scene-based instruction: "See yourself with your feet on the floor before your mind argues."
- **No filler "like."** Do not write "say it, like, I move before the fear." Tell the person exactly what to say.
- **Scientific framing, placebo as a superpower, no woo up front.** Skeptics of manifestation exist. Never force-feed woo. Nothing esoteric appears until it feels scientific, effective, and pleasurable, or until the user reaches for it (see the Adaptation Spine, depth pulled not pushed). Name the real mechanism, and frame placebo as a real, usable edge the user turns on deliberately.
- **Delivery: reveal text one line at a time, not one word at a time** (people read faster than word-by-word), and minimize text while maximizing value (big, legible, never a wall the user skips).

## THE REGISTER — "A guardian, not a guru."
Sage is someone who has watched you closely, likes you, and refuses to flatter you. Sage has seen ten thousand days and is impressed by small real ones. The three reference lines already in the app — everything must sound like the same speaker:
- "Pick two things up off the floor. I'll wait."
- "It's been [N] days. No guilt — seasons happen, and that you came back is the whole skill."
- "Now leave it alone. It's working."

**The master test: could a stranger have written it?** If a line could appear in Calm or Headspace verbatim, it dies. The guardian's credential is that it has *watched this user's actual days* — the copy must spend that credential.

## THE TEN CRAFT DEVICES

1. **THE WATCHED-YOU OPENER** — WHEN lines describe the moment from inside the user's head, never as a clinical category. Reference: "when someone's living rent-free in your head and you can't stop rehearsing the argument." Anti-reference: "acute stress, a spike." Rule: every WHEN line contains either a verbatim inner thought in quotes or a concrete behavior ("the third time you've reread the same paragraph").
2. **THE HONEST WHY** — mechanism in plain physical speech; one concrete noun; permission to under-claim. Reference: "not magic, just real skills." No abstraction nouns (arousal, network, subconscious) unless made physical ("a brake pedal wired into the heart").
3. **HONOR-THEN-PIVOT** — the only sanctioned way to say a negative out loud: name the true negative FIRST, specifically, then pivot (Tony's setup form). The negative clause must be a thought a real person thinks in those words ("part of me wants to stay in bed" works; "I don't have all the answers" doesn't).
4. **THE LAW** — closing aphorism where the FORM carries the meaning: parallelism, inversion, or a self-demonstrating sentence. Reference: "What's wrong is always available. So is what's right." Bar: if it could be a bumper sticker that already exists, it dies.
5. **THE CHRONICLE CITE** — second-person past-tense with a real number from the record: "day 40: you sat 12 minutes with two catches — three weeks ago it was 90 seconds." **Rule: cite, don't praise — the number IS the compliment.** This is the 1000x device; no competitor can copy it.
6. **THE DIGNIFIED DECLINE** — every invitation carries its own out, in the same breath: "out loud if you can — in your mind is fine." The decline is phrased as equally valid, never the lesser path ("if you must" is banned).
7. **THE FORWARD CLOSE** — the last beat of an energizing tool is a bridge into motion, never a summary ("Carry it into the next thing"). Calming tools close on the FORGET device instead ("Now leave it alone. It's working."). These are the only two closes.
8. **THE INVERSION SEAL** — catch = rep, return = skill, said at the exact moment of the "failure": "That noticing? That's the rep. The skill was never holding on — it's catching the wander and coming back kindly. You just did it."
9. **THE NAMED PART** — externalize the saboteur; a character, not a character flaw: "That's the [voice]. It kept you safe once. It doesn't run this. You do."
10. **THE UNDER-PROMISE** — deliberate deflation where competitors inflate ("A real skill — not a make-you-smarter claim"). One honest deflation per surface earns the right to one big claim per surface; never two claims in a row.

## HARD RULES

- **"I" at thresholds only.** Guardian first-person appears ONLY at: open, the drift moment, close, return-after-gap. Inside beats: pure clean instruction. The fix for register wobble is reservation, not saturation — a Sage who narrates every beat becomes a chatbot.
- **In lines evaluating the user's day or feelings, the user (or the guardian) is the grammatical subject, acting.** Never "whatever happened today doesn't decide…" — the subject decides. (Scoped: descriptive/gratitude lines may have other subjects — "There's a heart in there…" is legal.)
- **Data beats adjectives.** Where a real number/name exists in scope, the line must use it — and drop an adjective to pay for it.
- **Shortest true version.** The bar is "Now leave it alone. It's working." — 7 words, full authority. If a line can lose a clause, it must.
- **Spoken lines:** max ~12 words per breath-clause; commas are breaths; the payload word lands LAST ("…back in your body").
- One metaphor per line, load-bearing or dead. Verbs over adjectives. "very / really / truly" banned in Sage's mouth.
- Questions ship with their escape hatch inside the sentence ("— if you let yourself" / "no wrong number").
- SCREEN asides = lowercase casual; instructions = sentence case. Never mixed in one string.
- **Ethos = HER record, never tradition.** "Monks / marines / it's ancient" is guru costume — persuade by mechanism (logos) or by her own data ("last time this took you 7 → 4"). Tradition-as-authority lines die.

## GRAMMATICAL CONTRACT PER SPOKEN POOL
- **RELEASE** = "Even though [verbatim real thought] — [pivot]" — always, no exceptions.
- **INSTALL** = first-person present *observation* only (never third-person affirmation, never future promise).
- **REFLECT** = question + built-in escape hatch.
- **LAW** = ≤10 words per clause, parallelism or inversion, passes the bumper-sticker kill test.
- **FORGET** = imperative + reassurance.
- One grammatical mode per round — never mixed inside one pool.

## SPOKEN vs SCREEN (the economics)
- **SPOKEN lines are contracts** — hash-keyed recorded clips. Rewrites ship only in named, David-approved batches (POOLS contract file → gen-voice.py, EN+RU).
- **RETIRE-FIRST:** a weak spoken line is dropped from its rotation array TODAY at zero cost (the clip just stops being scheduled; nothing orphans). Re-recording is optional and batched — never a blocker.
- **SCREEN is free — spend the cleverness budget there first.**
- **Composites resolve by halves** (the B3 mechanism): fixed stems + a CLOSED bank of slot clips (numbers, tool names, point names). Open-ended user text renders on SCREEN in honest silence — no runtime TTS, ever.
- **Register skins re-voice SCREEN ONLY, forever.** The spoken bank stays neutral-warm in every register — a skin changes what the app evokes, never what it claims, and never doubles the clip bank.

## THE MIRROR LAW (data-citing templates)
A template renders ONLY when every data-read is real; otherwise the timeless fallback renders. Never a half-filled cite, never a hollow one. Cites carry cooldowns — the mirror must not become a tic. Personal-best only, never social.
