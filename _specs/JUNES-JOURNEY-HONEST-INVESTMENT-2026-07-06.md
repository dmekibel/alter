## What it is, and why it prints money
June's Journey (Wooga → Playtika), a 1920s hidden-object mystery. **$1B+ lifetime revenue, ~$280M/yr run-rate** (Playtika SEC filings), the #1 hidden-object game since 2019. **~85–90% women, plurality 55+.** The number that matters most: its retention curve is almost **flat** — D30 ≈16.5%, D60 ≈15%, **D180 ≈14.25%** (Naavik 2023). The category median craters to ~5% by D30. That ~2-point drop over *six months* is the quantitative signature of **investment-driven retention, not novelty-driven** — the players don't stay for fun, they stay for what they've *built*.

**Two design facts worth tattooing on the wall:**
1. **There is no lose condition anywhere in the game.** Deliberate, for this audience. No-fail = no negative-affect churn. (This is ALTER's `never-punish` law, proven at a billion dollars.)
2. **A new story chapter has shipped every week since 2018 without a miss** (275+ chapters). The flat retention is credited to this **weekly narrative drip**, not the mechanics. Story cadence is the real engine.

## Why the sister is hooked — the *real* psychology (all cited, all honest human wiring)
Her pride is **not** irrational or a trick. It's genuine psychology aimed at a fake object:

- **Extended self** (Belk, *Possessions and the Extended Self*, 1988): "you are what you own." A collection is **autobiography made tangible** — identity and self-narrative in physical form. Her island literally functions as a record of *her*.
- **IKEA effect** (Norton, Mochon & Ariely, 2012): we love what we *build with our own labor*. She hand-placed every decoration; the value is the effort, not the pixels.
- **Endowment effect** (Kahneman/Knetsch/Thaler): we overvalue what we own, simply because it's ours.
- **Sunk-cost / escalating commitment** (Staw 1976; Staw & Fox 1977): years of coins and time poured in — leaving "wastes" it all; quitting mid-ladder feels like abandoning a fight already won.
- **Goal-gradient** (Hull 1932; Kivetz 2006): effort visibly accelerates near a threshold. The game is *wall-to-wall progress bars* — flowers, league points, Help Wanted tiers, event checkpoints.
- **Identity fusion via social bond** (Naavik): reciprocal daily gifting in a 15-person Detective Club — leaving means abandoning *people*, not just progress.

**The mechanisms are neutral — they're how human value and attention actually work.** They only turn *manipulative* when the designer does one of three things (this is the whole ethical line):
1. **Manufactures expiry / artificial scarcity** (weaponizes loss-aversion).
2. **Gates completion behind real money.**
3. **Keeps the loop perpetually open** so relief never arrives.

## The honest ALTER translation — mechanic by mechanic

### The collection = the residue of real behavior (never buyable)
- The "collection" is earned **strictly through her real actions** — days lived, rituals kept, reflections written, skills matured. Never purchasable → it stays 100% honest autobiography (Belk). This IS the charged timeline + the chronicle ([[timeline-battery-principle]]): make the accumulated past **beautiful and browsable** — the reward for consistency is that *your own history becomes gorgeous to look at* ([[alter-profound-layer]] time-symmetry).
- **Make it a real display surface** — a garden / gallery / map / the scrollable charged timeline — that shows the accumulated *whole* back to her. Orchid Island's power is that the island IS the trophy case: permanent, visible, revisitable. ALTER's trophy case is her actual life.

### Let her curate, not just accumulate (the IKEA effect, honestly)
- June's Journey's limited plots force players to *swap and re-curate* — which is what makes the island feel *authored*, not hoarded. Let her arrange, theme, and retire pieces of her record → curation turns accumulation into **taste and identity**, and taps the IKEA effect legitimately (she built it).

### The daily deposit (honest, no wall)
- June's Journey's daily hooks: free daily flowers, daily gift, daily club drink, Help-Wanted board, contextual push. **Keep the daily-deposit ritual; drop the energy wall.** ALTER's honest daily deposit = the micro-stack / one real thing → the timeline visibly charges → a small somatic acknowledgment. The deposit is into *her* account, not the app's.
- **Refuse the energy wall entirely.** It has no in-fiction meaning; it exists only to create purchase friction. ALTER never gates *basic function* — the tools are timeless and free ([[alter-timeless-tools-and-monetization]]).

### The weekly rhythm + narrative drip (the real retention engine)
- The flat-retention secret is **weekly story**, not mechanics. ALTER's equivalent: the **journey / chronicle chapter** — the guardian narrates her arc on a weekly cadence (the week-3 mirror: "here's what I noticed this week"). Medium-term goals (a habit consolidating, a skill maturing) stacked on the daily deposits = the goal-gradient, pointed at *real thresholds* instead of a gacha pack.

### Mini-game variety (honest boredom-fighting)
- June's Journey reskins one core skill (visual search) into Time Rush / Spot-the-Difference / Pairs / Word Jumble to fight boredom. ALTER's honest version: vary the *frame* of the daily ritual (a breath, a reflection, a tiny plan, a noticing) so the daily deposit never feels identical — variety of surface, same real substance.

### Social — real bonds, not synthetic guilt
- June's Journey's club creates identity-fusion but also *synthetic obligation* (guilt about missing a stranger's timed drink). If ALTER ever adds social, connect her to **real people whose accountability matters after the app** (family, friends — the persona network), never manufactured guilt about a stranger's timer.

## Monetization — the honest half of a billion-dollar model
David **will** charge (model TBD). June's Journey shows exactly which half to copy:

**ADOPT (the honest half):**
- A **genuinely free, complete core** so paying is for **more, never to unblock** basic function. (ALTER's timeless tools = free forever.)
- **Tiered optional depth bought once (or a real subscription-for-value):** collection expansions, seasonal content packs, guardian cosmetics, deeper KB/grimoire chapters.
- **One transparent currency, or none.** Flat, honest pricing. No countdowns.
- Optional upsells with **obvious immediate value** (June's Journey's rewarded-ad "10 vs 15 energy" is an honest exchange).

**REFUSE (the dark half — the refusal list, extended):**
- The **energy/wait wall** (artificial scarcity to force spend).
- **Loot boxes / gacha** (Star Boxes $7.99–$99.99 — variable-reward gambling; especially predatory on compulsive spenders).
- **Dual currency** (diamonds + coins — engineered to obscure true cost).
- **FOMO countdowns** on infinitely-reproducible digital goods (the 24h Day-5 bundle).
- **Guilt-dismiss copy** ("maybe later / show again" instead of "no thanks").
- **Whale price-discrimination** (industry-standard, unconfirmed for JJ — a hard red line regardless).
- **Loss-aversion on the collection itself** ("your streak/collection disappears if you stop") — the fastest way to convert healthy pride into anxious compulsion (the Psychology-Today line between collecting and hoarding-disorder).

**The one rule that separates the two halves:** June's Journey needs her to *keep depositing forever into an account it controls and can devalue at will*. ALTER's deposits must belong to **her** categorically — meaningful, exportable, and true even if she quits tomorrow. **Use the investment psychology; refuse investment in something fake and rented.**
